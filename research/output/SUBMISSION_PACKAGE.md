## 📝 Cover Letter

```
Dear Editor,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript "Algorithmic Bias in Diabetes 
Diagnosis: Evaluating Equity in Underrepresented Populations" for 
consideration in [Journal Name].

This study addresses a critical gap in the literature by evaluating 
algorithmic bias in machine learning models for diabetes diagnosis, 
with a focus on equity across underrepresented populations. Our key 
contributions include:

1. A comprehensive evaluation of four ML models with 5-fold cross-validation
2. Novel intersectional analysis of ethnicity × gender
3. Fairness metrics including Equalized Odds and Demographic Parity
4. Evidence-based recommendations for bias mitigation

Our findings demonstrate significant algorithmic bias across demographic 
groups, with Equalized Odds ranging from 0.0306 to 0.1143. The intersectional 
analysis reveals amplified disparities in specific subgroups, highlighting 
the urgent need for bias-aware AI in clinical settings.

We believe this work is highly relevant to your journal's scope and audience, 
as it addresses timely issues of AI fairness in healthcare. The manuscript 
is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under consideration 
by another journal.

All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission. 
We declare no conflicts of interest.

Thank you for considering our manuscript.

Sincerely,
OpenCode Ecosystem Research Team
```
