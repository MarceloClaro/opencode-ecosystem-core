<header><h1>&lt;TransitionSeries><a target=_blank style=text-decoration:none;font-size:16px;margin-left:8px href=https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion/releases/v4.0.59><span style="font-size:0.9em;font-feature-settings:'ss03' on;border:1px solid var(--ifm-color-emphasis-300);color:var(--ifm-color-emphasis-600);font-family:GTPlanar;padding-left:6px;padding-right:6px;border-radius:3px;padding-top:2px;padding-bottom:2px" title="Added in v4.0.59">v<!-- -->4.0.59</span></a></h1></header>
<p>The <code>&lt;TransitionSeries></code> component behaves the same as the <a class="" href=/docs/series><code>&lt;Series></code></a> component, but allows for <a href=#transitionseriestransition class=""><code>&lt;TransitionSeries.Transition></code></a> and <a href=#transitionseriesoverlay class=""><code>&lt;TransitionSeries.Overlay></code></a> components to be rendered between <a href=#transitionseriessequence class=""><code>&lt;TransitionSeries.Sequence></code></a> components.</p>
<p>Between any two sequences, you can place either a transition or an overlay:</p>
<ul>
<li class="">A <a href=#transitionseriestransition class=""><code>&lt;TransitionSeries.Transition></code></a> crossfades, slides, or otherwise animates between two scenes.<br/>
<!-- -->It <a href=#duration-of-a-transitionseries class="">shortens</a> the total duration because both scenes overlap during the transition.</li>
<li class="">A <a href=#transitionseriesoverlay class=""><code>&lt;TransitionSeries.Overlay></code></a> renders children on top of the cut point without affecting timing.<br/>
<!-- -->The sequences remain at full length — useful for effects like <a class="" href=/docs/light-leaks/light-leak>light leaks</a> or flashes.</li>
</ul>

<p>The <code>&lt;TransitionSeries></code> component can only contain <code>&lt;TransitionSeries.Sequence></code>, <code>&lt;TransitionSeries.Transition></code>, and <code>&lt;TransitionSeries.Overlay></code> components.<br/>
<code>layout="none"</code> is deprecated and throws from Remotion 5.0.0 on.<br/>
<!-- -->Transition scenes must stay absolutely positioned. Remove the <code>layout</code> prop or keep the default <code>absolute-fill</code>. See the <a class="" href=/docs/5-0-migration>v5 migration guide</a>.</p>
<h4 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_rM1N" id=durationinframes><code>durationInFrames?</code><a target=_blank style=text-decoration:none;font-size:16px;margin-left:8px href=https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion/releases/v4.0.533><span style="font-size:0.9em;font-feature-settings:'ss03' on;border:1px solid var(--ifm-color-emphasis-300);color:var(--ifm-color-emphasis-600);font-family:GTPlanar;padding-left:6px;padding-right:6px;border-radius:3px;padding-top:2px;padding-bottom:2px" title="Added in v4.0.533">v<!-- -->4.0.533</span></a><a href=#durationinframes class=hash-link aria-label="Direct link to durationinframes" title="Direct link to durationinframes" translate=no>​</a></h4>
<p>Limits how long the entire <code>&lt;TransitionSeries></code> is rendered, using the same behavior as <a class="" href=/docs/sequence#durationinframes><code>&lt;Sequence durationInFrames></code></a>. It does not change the durations of its scenes or transitions.</p>
<p>By default, the wrapper lasts until the end of its parent sequence or composition. In the Studio, its timeline bar ends at the latest child end, accounting for transition overlaps, offsets, and playback rates. Dragging the wrapper’s right edge before that end writes an explicit <code>durationInFrames</code>. Dragging it to or beyond that end clamps the edge there and removes <code>durationInFrames</code> on release, restoring the inferred bar length.</p>
<h3 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_rM1N" id=transitionseriessequence><code>&lt;TransitionSeries.Sequence></code><a href=#transitionseriessequence class=hash-link aria-label="Direct link to transitionseriessequence" title="Direct link to transitionseriessequence" translate=no>​</a></h3>
<p>Inherits the <a class="" href=/docs/sequence#durationinframes><code>durationInFrames</code></a>, <a class="" href=/docs/sequence#playbackrate><code>playbackRate</code></a>, <a class="" href=/docs/sequence#name><code>name</code></a>, <a class="" href=/docs/sequence#classname><code>className</code></a>, <a class="" href=/docs/sequence#style><code>style</code></a>, <a class="" href=/docs/sequence#showintimeline><code>showInTimeline</code></a>, <a class="" href=/docs/sequence#freeze><code>freeze</code></a>, <a class="" href=/docs/sequence#premountfor><code>premountFor</code></a> and <a class="" href=/docs/sequence#postmountfor><code>postmountFor</code></a> props from <a class="" href=/docs/sequence><code>&lt;Sequence></code></a>.</p>
<p><code>layout="none"</code> is deprecated and throws from Remotion 5.0.0 on.<br/>
<!-- -->Transition scenes must stay absolutely positioned. Remove the <code>layout</code> prop or keep the default <code>absolute-fill</code>. See the <a class="" href=/docs/5-0-migration>v5 migration guide</a>.</p>
<p>The <a class="" href=/docs/sequence#trimbefore><code>trimBefore</code></a> prop is supported from <a target=_blank style=text-decoration:none;font-size:16px;margin-left:8px href=https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion/releases/v4.0.497><span style="font-size:0.9em;font-feature-settings:'ss03' on;border:1px solid var(--ifm-color-emphasis-300);color:var(--ifm-color-emphasis-600);font-family:GTPlanar;padding-left:6px;padding-right:6px;border-radius:3px;padding-top:2px;padding-bottom:2px" title="Added in v4.0.497">v<!-- -->4.0.497</span></a>.</p>
<p>As children, put the contents of your scene.</p>
<h3 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_rM1N" id=transitionseriestransition><code>&lt;TransitionSeries.Transition></code><a href=#transitionseriestransition class=hash-link aria-label="Direct link to transitionseriestransition" title="Direct link to transitionseriestransition" translate=no>​</a></h3>
<p>Placed between two sequences to animate from one scene to the next.<br/>
<!-- -->During the transition, both scenes are rendered simultaneously and the total duration is <a href=#duration-of-a-transitionseries class="">shortened</a> by the transition length.</p>
<p>Takes two props:</p>
<ul>
<li class=""><code>timing</code>: A timing of type <code>TransitionTiming</code>. See <a class="" href=/docs/transitions/timings>Timings</a> for more information.</li>
<li class=""><code>presentation?</code>: A presentation of type <code>TransitionPresentation</code>. If not specified, the default value is <a class="" href=/docs/transitions/presentations/slide><code>slide()</code></a>. See <a class="" href=/docs/transitions/presentations>Presentations</a> for more information.</li>
</ul>
<p>Must be a direct child of <code>&lt;TransitionSeries></code>.
At least one <code>&lt;TransitionSeries.Sequence></code> component must come before or after the <code>&lt;TransitionSeries.Transition></code> component.</p>
<h3 class="anchor anchorTargetStickyNavbar_rM1N" id=transitionseriesoverlay><code>&lt;TransitionSeries.Overlay></code><a target=_blank style=text-decoration:none;font-size:16px;margin-left:8px href=https://github.com/remotion-dev/remotion/releases/v4.0.415><span style="font-size:0.9em;font-feature-settings:'ss03' on;border:1px solid var(--ifm-color-emphasis-300);color:var(--ifm-color-emphasis-600);font-family:GTPlanar;padding-left:6px;padding-right:6px;border-radius:3px;padding-top:2px;padding-bottom:2px" title="Added in v4.0.415">v<!-- -->4.0.415</span></a><a href=#transitionseriesoverlay class=hash-link aria-label="Direct link to transitionseriesoverlay" title="Direct link to transitionseriesoverlay" translate=no>​</a></h3>
<p>Placed between two sequences to render children on top of the cut point.<br/>
<!-- -->The overlay does <a href=#duration-of-a-transitionseries class="">not shorten</a> the timeline — adjacent sequences remain at full length.</p>
<p>The overlay is centered on the cut point by default: half the duration before the cut, half after.<br/>
<!-- -->Children animate independently — no progress context is provided.</p>
<p>Takes two props:</p>
<ul>
<li class=""><code>durationInFrames</code>: How long the overlay is visible. Must be a positive integer.</li>
<li class=""><code>offset?</code>: Shifts the overlay relative to the center of the cut point. Positive values shift right (later), negative values shift left (earlier). Default: <code>0</code>. Must be a finite integer.</li>
</ul>
<p>The overlay must not extend before frame 0 or beyond the duration of the adjacent sequences.</p>
