## Note on the philosophical case study

The article analyzes a system created and used by its author. The health information in the manuscript is a self-report, not a diagnosis. The article does not offer medical advice and does not claim that the system prevents, treats, or diagnoses Alzheimer's disease or any other condition. The author's decision to make this information public is intentional and is limited to what is necessary to explain the philosophical problem. No clinical records, biomarkers, or third-party data are included.
