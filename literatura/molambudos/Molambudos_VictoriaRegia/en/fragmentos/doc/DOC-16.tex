\section*{\textbf{DOC-16} --- Oliveira's Diary VI}
\markboth{DOC-16 --- Oliveira's Diary VI}{}

\noindent \textit{The last record}

\noindent \textbf{Dr. Heitor Oliveira --- Personal Notebook}
\textbf{--- --- --- ---}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent \textit{This record was found inside patient 1,260's diary, in 2026, when the diary was returned to the Minas Gerais Public Archive. The handwriting is Oliveira's, but the last lines alternate between his hand and Joaquim's. The date is illegible.}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent I do not know what day it is.

\noindent I lost count after the trench.

\noindent I am in Quixeramobim. At a boarding house. The room is small, it has a bed, a lamp, and the smell. The smell is everywhere now. On my clothes. On my skin. On the food. The food tastes sweet. Everything tastes sweet.

\noindent The right eye is completely yellow.

\noindent I do not look in the mirror.

\noindent The diary writes on its own now. I do not touch it. It is on the table. The pages turn by themselves. The handwriting appears. I read.

\noindent It wrote:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Heitor. You are ready.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I answered: \textquotedblleft{}Ready for what?\textquotedblright{}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}To accept.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textquotedblleft{}Accept what?\textquotedblright{}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}That you are no longer a doctor. You are a patient. Patient 1,261. The number is not Joaquim's. It is the creature's. She counts the bodies she has occupied. You are next in line.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I picked up a pen. I wrote over it:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I do not accept.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The diary answered:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You do not need to accept. You only need to exist. The creature lives in you. Accepting or not.\textquotedblright{}}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent This morning, a woman knocked on the door.

\noindent She was elderly. White hair. A tired face. She looked at me and said:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Are you the doctor?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}...I was.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Did you read the diary?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}How do you know?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The eye. The yellow. Everyone who has read it gets the eye.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Who are you?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She sat on the bed. Without asking.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}My name is Maria do Rosário. I am Joaquim's mother.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent My mouth fell open.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You are alive?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I was. I died in 1965. But that does not matter. What matters is that I came to warn you.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Warn me about what?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The diary cannot stay with you. You have to return it.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Return it where?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}To the archive. So that another person reads it.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Another person? To contaminate someone else?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She looked at me. Her eyes were tired.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Do you think I did not try? When I found out Joaquim was alive, in 1950, I came to visit. He gave me the diary. He said: 'Read it, Mother. So you know who I am.' I read it. I spent a week with him. Then I returned it. I said I did not want to know.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Did you feel the smell?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I did.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The eye?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It lightened. But it passed. I passed the diary on. I gave it to the gravedigger. He buried it with Joaquim. I thought it was over.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It was not.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}No. The gravedigger read it. Then he returned it to the archive. Then came Dr. Tavares. Then Dr. Verônica. Then you.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She stood up.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You are not the first, Doctor. You will not be the last.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What do I do?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She walked to the door.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Pass it on.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}To whom?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}To someone who needs to know. To someone who can tell the story.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And if I do not pass it on?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She looked at me. For the last time.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Then you die with her inside you. And the Cycle waits. Until the next reader.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She left.

\noindent I never saw her again.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent Today is --- I do not know.

\noindent The diary is open. I did not touch it. But it wrote:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Go back to Barbacena. Return the diary. Let another read it. It is the only way out.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I will do that.

\noindent I will return it.

\noindent But first, I will write everything.

\noindent So that whoever reads after me will know.

\noindent \textbf{The Cycle does not end.}
\textbf{The Cycle repeats.}
\textbf{Patient 1,260 died.}
\textbf{Patient 1,261 is me.}
\textbf{Patient 1,262 will be another.}

\noindent \textit{--- Heitor Oliveira}
\textit{--- Patient 1,261}
\textit{--- Former doctor}
\textit{--- Former man}
\textit{--- Former everything}
