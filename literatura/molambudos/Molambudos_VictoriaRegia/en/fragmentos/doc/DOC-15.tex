\section*{\textbf{DOC-15} --- Oliveira's Diary V}
\markboth{DOC-15 --- Oliveira's Diary V}{}

\noindent \textit{The trench exists}

\noindent \textbf{Dr. Heitor Oliveira --- Personal Notebook}
\textbf{June 17, 1979 --- Fortaleza, Ceará}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent I found it.

\noindent The trench exists.

\noindent I spent three days looking. The boundaries of the former Alagadiço Camp, on the outskirts of Fortaleza, were no longer legible in the landscape. Without specialized research, the traces were difficult to locate.

\noindent But a 90-year-old man, sitting in a Fortaleza neighborhood, said:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The trench? I know where it is. It is behind what used to be the pen. There is nothing there. Just brush. But the brush does not grow right. The earth is black. And it stinks.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He took me there.

\noindent We walked 20 minutes along a dirt road. Then brush. Then a clearing.

\noindent The earth was black. Oily. The sweetish smell rose from the ground like vapor.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Here\textquotedblright{}\textit{, said the old man. }\textquotedblleft{}They buried the molambudos here. Hundreds. Thousands. No one knows how many.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}How do you know?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}My father worked at the pen. He said the soldiers threw the bodies here. At night. Without praying. Without anything.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And nothing ever grew again?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It grows. But it grows crooked. Look.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He pointed to a tree in the middle of the clearing. The trunk was twisted, the branches grew downward. The leaves were dark.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}That tree grew there. It drank from the black earth. Look at the color of the leaves. It looks like... dried blood.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I paid the old man. He left.

\noindent I was alone in the clearing.

\noindent The sweetish smell was strong. Very strong. Like in the cell. Like in my room.

\noindent I sat on the ground. The earth was soft. Loose. As if it were full of holes.

\noindent I put my hand in the black earth.

\noindent It was warm.

\noindent I took the diary out of the backpack. I opened it to the last page.

\noindent The handwriting was forming on its own:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You made it.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Yes\textquotedblright{}}, I said out loud.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What did you expect to find?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The truth.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The truth is that you will never leave here.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I looked around. The clearing. The crooked tree. The black earth. The smell.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}No\textquotedblright{}\textit{, I said. }\textquotedblleft{}I am leaving. I am going home. I will burn this diary. I will forget.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You will not.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I will.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I fell silent.

\noindent The creature inside me laughed.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You are already mine, Heitor. From the first page. The Cycle has no way back.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I sat at the trench until sunset.

\noindent When I looked at the sky, the Shroud-Ripper was there. Perched on a dead tree. Looking at me.

\noindent I kept looking back.

\noindent Until the dark swallowed everything.
