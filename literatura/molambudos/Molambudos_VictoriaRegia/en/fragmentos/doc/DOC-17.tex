\section*{\textbf{DOC-17} --- A Soldier's Letter}
\markboth{DOC-17 --- A Soldier's Letter}{}

\noindent \textit{1915 --- the pen seen from within}

\noindent \textbf{Fictional reconstruction --- narrative document}
\textbf{1915 --- Fictional letter with no known archival counterpart}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent \textit{Fictional letter attributed to Soldier Antônio Alves Neto, stationed at the Alagadiço Camp, near Fortaleza.}

\noindent \textit{Fictional reconstruction addressed to his wife, Maria, in Sobral; it has not been located in military archives.}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent \textit{Maria,}

\noindent \textit{I am writing this letter in the pen's tent, but I do not know if I will be able to send it. The situation here is worse than the colonel tells the press.}

\noindent \textit{Every day more retirantes arrive. Whole families. People who walked for days without food or water. The children arrive with sunken eyes and swollen bellies. The women, when they arrive, are separated and taken for \textquotedblleft{}relocation\textquotedblright{}. I know what that means. Everyone knows.}

\noindent \textit{The men and the children stay. They stay behind the barbed wire, waiting for the food that never comes.}

\noindent \textit{The government sends cattle, but the cattle goes to our kitchen, not to theirs. They receive a thin broth, once a day. It is enough to keep them from dying all at once. It is enough to make them die slowly.}

\noindent \textit{The worst are the children. There is a boy here, no more than ten, who arrived alone. His mother was taken away on the truck. His father died on the road. He does not cry. He only stares. His eye is yellow. The right one. He said \textquotedblleft{}a beast\textquotedblright{} changed him. He does not cry, Maria. He only stares. And that stare... it cuts through me.}

\noindent \textit{Last week, I saw something at the trench.}

\noindent \textit{The trench of the bodies, behind the pen. I was on night guard and I heard a noise. Chewing. I went to look.}

\noindent \textit{There is a thing in there. It lives among the bodies. It is big. Very big. It has yellow eyes that shine in the dark. It did not attack me. It only looked. And went on eating.}

\noindent \textit{I told the sergeant. He said it is \textquotedblleft{}imagination\textquotedblright{} and \textquotedblleft{}hunger\textquotedblright{}. He said hunger makes you see things.}

\noindent \textit{Maybe it is. Maybe it is not.}

\noindent \textit{But tonight, when I close my eyes, I will see the yellow eyes.}

\noindent \textit{I saw them.}

\noindent \textit{And, Maria, they saw me too.}

\noindent \textit{--- Antonio}
