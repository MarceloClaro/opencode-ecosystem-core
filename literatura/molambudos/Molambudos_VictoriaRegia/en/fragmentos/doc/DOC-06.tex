\section*{\textbf{DOC-06} --- Session Report III}
\markboth{DOC-06 --- Session Report III}{}

\noindent \textit{Dr. Heitor Oliveira --- April 2, 1979}

\noindent \textbf{Dr. Heitor Oliveira --- April 2, 1979}
\textit{Last report. Session interrupted.}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent \textbf{Patient:} Joaquim Antônio Correia --- Patient 1,260
\textbf{Type:} Psychiatric assessment session
\textbf{Duration:} 20 minutes (session interrupted)

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent \textbf{INITIAL OBSERVATION:}

\noindent This session was interrupted by an incident. The patient presented a sudden change in behavior, and the session was ended for safety.

\noindent Account follows:

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent \textbf{Account:}

\noindent The session began normally. Joaquim was calm. He handed me the diary --- something he had been refusing to do for two weeks. He placed it on the table and said: \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Read.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I asked if he was sure. He said yes.

\noindent I opened the diary. The first pages were written in childish handwriting, with spelling errors and short sentences. The pages described the routine at the Colony. Nothing extraordinary.

\noindent But the last pages ---

\noindent The last pages were written in a different hand. An adult hand, firm, in an almost cursive script. They described the \textquotedblleft{}creature\textquotedblright{}, the \textquotedblleft{}trench\textquotedblright{}, the \textquotedblleft{}cycle\textquotedblright{\textit{. They described }this office\textit{. They described }this session}.

\noindent There was a passage:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The doctor will read this diary. He will smell it. He will be afraid. He will deny it. But the cycle will begin.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent When I looked up, Joaquim was no longer in the chair.

\noindent He was in the corner of the room, squatting, his arms wrapped around his legs. His eyes --- the right eye --- was glowing.

\noindent I called his name.

\noindent The voice that answered was not his.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent This is the hard part to report.

\noindent The voice was deeper. Slower. As if it came from a larger body. It said:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}He is no longer here.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I asked who was speaking.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You know who.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The creature. The patient was in an acute psychotic crisis. The delusional entity was manifesting itself.

\noindent I tried to stay calm.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What do you want?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Nothing. I only want you to see.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}See what?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What every doctor refuses to see.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent At this moment, the patient began to tremble. His whole body shook. His mouth opened. His eyes rolled back. I thought it was a seizure.

\noindent It was not.

\noindent He stopped trembling. He raised his head. The right eye was \textit{lit} --- that is not a metaphor. There was luminescence.

\noindent He smiled.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You think it is a delusion?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I did not answer.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You think it is trauma? Dissociation? Projection?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I still did not answer.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Then explain one thing to me, Doctor.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He stood up. He came toward me. He did not touch me. He stopped a few centimeters away.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Why did you dream of the trench before reading the diary?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I had not told him. I had not told anyone.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Why does his eye glow in the dark?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}That is...\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What? Physiology? Pathology?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He laughed. The laugh was not human.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You have a word for everything. Schizophrenia. Trauma. Delusion. Dissociation. You name what you do not understand and think you have understood.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He leaned in. The smell --- the sweetish smell --- was strong.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The name is not the thing, Doctor. It is one thing not to know the name. It is another to think the name is the explanation.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent At this moment, I turned off the light in the room and said the session was over. The patient did not react. He went back to the chair and sat down as if nothing had happened.

\noindent When I asked if he remembered what had happened, he said:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I remember. But it was not me.\textquotedblright{}}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent \textbf{Therapeutic Plan:}

\noindent - Patient presents psychotic exacerbation. Increase Chlorpromazine to 200mg/day.
- Request neurological evaluation (rule out temporal lobe epilepsy).
- Sessions suspended temporarily.

\noindent \textbf{Personal Note:}

\noindent I am not going to sleep tonight.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent \textit{Dr. Heitor Oliveira}
\textit{Psychiatrist --- Maximum Security Ward}
\textit{Barbacena Hospital-Colony}

\noindent \textit{P.S. --- Tonight, when I got home, the diary was in my bag. I did not put it there. I do not know how it got there.}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\subsection*{ADDENDUM --- Written on April 3, 1979 (1 day after the session)}

\noindent I did not sleep.

\noindent I tried. I lay down. I closed my eyes. But the smell was on the pillow. The sweetish smell. I got up and washed my face. The smell did not go away. It was on my skin.

\noindent I opened the diary again. I read the passage about the trench. When I looked up, it was 3:14 in the morning. The same hour Joaquim says he wakes up.

\noindent I looked at the corner of the room. There was nothing there. But I \textit{almost} saw it. A shadow. A movement. A glow.

\noindent I closed the diary. I put it inside the drawer. I locked the drawer.

\noindent I lay down again.

\noindent The smell was still there.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\subsection*{ADDENDUM 2 --- April 5, 1979 (72h after the session)}

\noindent I dreamed of the trench.

\noindent It was not a dream. It was a \textit{visit}. I was lying on the pile of bodies. Their eyes were open. All of them looking at me. The creature was on top of me. Her face close to mine. The sweet breath.

\noindent She said: \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You read. Now you are mine.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I woke up screaming.

\noindent My wife turned on the light. She asked what was wrong. I said it was nothing. She said I was white as a sheet. I said I was fine.

\noindent A lie.

\noindent I am not fine.

\noindent The diary is in my head. The words will not leave. They repeat. All the time. Even with my eyes open.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You read. Now you are mine.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I need to sleep. I need to stop thinking. I need ---

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\subsection*{ADDENDUM 3 --- April 8, 1979}

\noindent Patient 1,260 no longer speaks.

\noindent I go to his cell every day. He is sitting on the bed, with the diary on his lap, staring at the wall. He does not look at me when I come in. He does not answer when I call him.

\noindent But his eye --- the yellow eye --- follows me.

\noindent No matter where I am in the cell. The eye tracks me. As if the eye were an animal separate from his body.

\noindent I asked the head nurse: \textquotedblleft{}Is patient 1,260 taking his medication?\textquotedblright{}

\noindent She said yes. Chlorpromazine 200mg. Twice a day.

\noindent Then why does his eye still glow?

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\subsection*{ADDENDUM 4 --- April 10, 1979}

\noindent I dreamed again.

\noindent This time I was not in the trench. I was in my consulting room. Patient 1,260 was sitting in the chair. But his face --- when he raised his head --- the face was mine.

\noindent I woke up to my own scream.

\noindent My wife sleeps on the sofa now. She said I talk in my sleep. I asked what I say. She said she does not want to repeat it.

\noindent I asked if I said \textquotedblleft{}yellow eye\textquotedblright{}.

\noindent She did not answer.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\subsection*{ADDENDUM 5 --- April 12, 1979}

\noindent I know the eye is not a delusion.

\noindent The eye glows. I saw it. Tonight, I went to the hospital. I entered the ward of the silenced. I stood at the door of cell 1,260. The patient was sleeping.

\noindent The right eye was open.

\noindent Glowing in the dark.

\noindent It is impossible. It is not physiology. It is not pathology. It is something else.

\noindent What is it?

\noindent What is it?

\noindent What is it?

\noindent \textit{I need to know what it is I need to know what it is I need to know what it is I need to know what it is}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\subsection*{ADDENDUM 6 --- April 13, 1979}

\noindent I locked the diary in the hospital cabinet.
I threw the key in the trash.
I went home.
The diary was on the kitchen table.

\noindent It will not stay away from me.

\noindent It \textit{wants} to be read.

\noindent It \textit{wants} to be passed on.

\noindent I will not write in this report anymore.

\noindent I will write in the diary.

\noindent It called me.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent \textit{Note from the administration: Dr. Heitor Oliveira did not report to work from April 14, 1979 onward.}
\textit{Attempts at telephone contact failed.}
\textit{A visit to his residence on April 16 found the house empty.}
\textit{Patient 1,260's diary was not found at the hospital.}
\textit{Dr. Oliveira remains missing.}
