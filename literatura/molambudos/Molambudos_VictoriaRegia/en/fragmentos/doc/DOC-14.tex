\markboth{DOC-14 --- Oliveira's Diary IV}{}

\noindent \textit{The flight}

\noindent \textbf{Dr. Heitor Oliveira --- Last Entry in Barbacena}
\textbf{June 13, 1979}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent I need to flee.

\noindent I am not leaving the diary. The diary comes with me. I know it will not stay away. I tried to leave it. It comes back. It always comes back.

\noindent I am writing in the car. Parked in front of the house. My wife is sleeping. I did not wake her to say goodbye. What would I say? \textquotedblleft{}Darling, I am leaving because a monster with yellow eyes entered me through a 1917 diary\textquotedblright{}?

\noindent She is already frightened. She has been sleeping on the sofa for weeks. She said I talk in my sleep. She said I cry in my sleep. She said I wake up with my right eye open and glowing.

\noindent She knows.
She just does not want to accept it.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent I am going north. To Ceará. To Quixeramobim.

\noindent I need to see the trench. I need to understand what happened there. I need to know whether the creature is real or a construction of my unconscious.

\noindent If it is real, the trench will have something. A sign. A mark.

\noindent If it is not...

\noindent If it is not, then I am mad. And madmen go to the Colony. And I am not going back to the Colony. Never again.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent \textit{--- I filled the car. 30 liters. Should last until Montes Claros.}

\noindent \textit{--- The diary is on the passenger seat. Open. The pages are filling in on their own. The handwriting is mine. But I am not writing.}

\noindent \textit{--- I am afraid.}

\noindent \textit{--- For the first time in my life, I am truly afraid.}

\noindent \textit{--- Not of a patient. Not of a disease. Not of death.}

\noindent \textit{--- Afraid of myself.}

\noindent \textit{--- Heitor Oliveira, June 13, 1979, 11:47 p.m.}
\textit{--- Leaving for Ceará. If anyone reads this: do not read the diary. Burn it. Bury it. But do not read it.}

\noindent \textit{--- Do not read it.}

\noindent \textit{--- Do not re---}
