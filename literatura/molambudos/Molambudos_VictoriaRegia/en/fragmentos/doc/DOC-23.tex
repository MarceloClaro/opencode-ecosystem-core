\noindent {\footnotesize\texttt{PUBLIC ARCHIVE $\cdot$ DOCUMENTARY EVIDENCE $\cdot$ CASE 1,260}}\\[-0.2em]

\noindent \textbf{Minas Gerais Public Archive --- Internal Security Report}\\
\noindent \textbf{Record No.:} 2026-RO-0847\\
\noindent\textbf{Date:} August 3, 2026. \textbf{Time:} 8:15 a.m.

\noindent \textbf{Requesting party:} Special Collection Management.\\
\noindent \textbf{Location:} Vault Room --- Basement, wing C.

\noindent \textbf{Account of the incident:}

\noindent At 7:50 a.m. on August 3, the guard on duty --- Edson Moreira, night shift --- reported to the control center that the weight sensor of the definitive vault had gone off during the night. The sensor registers changes in the total mass of the contents. The alarm sounded at 3:14 a.m., lasted six seconds, and silenced on its own. The control center did not register a guard being dispatched to the location because the weight sensor alarm protocol requires visual confirmation, but the vault's internal camera showed no image different from the baseline during the trigger.

\noindent At 8:00 a.m., the collection manager and the security technician opened the vault for inspection.

\noindent \textbf{Findings:}

\noindent a) Object 1,260 was in its original position, inside the conservation wrapper;

\noindent b) The security seal of the packaging was intact and corresponded to the number registered at the time of sealing (seal No. APM-2026-4471);

\noindent c) The room's precision scale indicated that the object \textbf{weighed 12 grams more} than recorded upon entering the vault, on July 28, 2026. The equipment's margin of error is 0.2 grams.

\noindent \textbf{Measures taken:}

\noindent a) Duplicate weighing on an independent scale --- confirmed: 12 additional grams;

\noindent b) Re-sealing of the packaging, with photographic record of the weight and the previous seal;

\noindent c) Notification of the directorate, which ordered administrative silence regarding the incident;

\noindent d) Replacement of the night-shift guard, at his own request. Edson Moreira declared, upon his dismissal: \textquotedblleft{}It was not the scale. It was the object moving inside the wrapper. I heard it. Paper moving does not make noise on its own.\textquotedblright{}

\noindent Before signing the termination form, Edson added --- and the security technician recorded the statement, as a precaution: \textquotedblleft{}I have two children and a wife at home. I am not going back into that room at night for any salary. Call it damp, call it a sensor fault, call it whatever you like. I have decided I would rather not know what it is. I would rather sleep soundly.\textquotedblright{} His fear did not fit any field on the standard form, but the collection manager noted, by hand, in the margin of the filed copy: \textquotedblleft{}He was not lying.\textquotedblright{}

\noindent \textbf{Filed on:} 08/03/2026, as determined by the directorate. Restricted access.
