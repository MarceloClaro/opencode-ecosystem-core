\subsection*{CLINICAL NOTES (transcription)}

\noindent \textbf{Dr. Nelson Fontes, 1937:}
\textit{\textquotedblleft{}Patient does not respond to therapy. Delusion remains unchanged. Increase voltage to 130V.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textbf{Dr. Amílcar Barbosa, 1940:}
\textit{\textquotedblleft{}After 18 sessions, the patient began reporting 'out-of-body experiences' during the shock. I consider this an advance --- the mind is detaching itself from the body. Continue.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textbf{Dr. Amílcar Barbosa, 1945:}
\textit{\textquotedblleft{}Femur fracture during session. The patient did not feel the fracture --- he reported only that he 'heard the bone break but felt no pain'. I suggest increasing the voltage. The patient's tolerance is exceptional.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textbf{Dr. Heitor Oliveira, 1979 (marginal note, 30 years later):}
\textit{\textquotedblleft{}This record is a torture document. 37 sessions without anesthesia. Untreated fractures. No consent. And the patient survived it all --- lucid enough to write a 62-year diary. That is not refractoriness. That is resilience. Or possession. Or both.\textquotedblright{}}

\subsection*{ARCHIVIST'S NOTE (2026)}

\noindent The Konvulsator Model IV was designed for use with anesthesia and muscle relaxant. At the Colony, it was used with neither. The maximum voltage recommended by the manufacturer was 130V. Patient 1,260 received up to 170V in the last session.

\noindent The patient's survival without apparent brain damage is \textquotedblleft{}clinically inexplicable\textquotedblright{} (Dr. Torres, 2023).

\noindent Patient 1,260 declared, in his diary (1952): \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The shock does not affect the creature. The shock burns pieces of me. The creature eats what is left.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent It is suggested that the patient's \textquotedblleft{}inexplicable\textquotedblright{} tolerance to the treatment may be related to the content of his delusion --- specifically, to the belief in an internal entity that absorbed the trauma.

\noindent Or, alternatively, that the delusion was not a delusion.

\noindent The Archive takes no position on this latter hypothesis.
