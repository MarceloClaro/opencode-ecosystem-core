\section*{\textbf{DOC-13} --- Oliveira's Diary III}
\markboth{DOC-13 --- Oliveira's Diary III}{}

\noindent \textit{The last session with Joaquim}

\noindent \textbf{Dr. Heitor Oliveira --- Personal Notebook}
\textbf{June 12, 1979}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent \textit{Archivist's note: This is the last entry written by Dr. Oliveira before the death of patient 1,260. The handwriting is visibly altered. Some words are crossed out. Others were written with such force that they tore through the paper.}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent HE DIED.

\noindent I am writing in the hospital corridor, with my hands dirty with blood and dirt. No, it is not blood. It is ink. The ink of the diary leaked. Or I dirtied my hand on the wall. I do not know.

\noindent Patient 1,260 died today.

\noindent He was in the session. He was calm. Calmer than ever. He looked at me with the yellow eye --- the same eye that is now on my face --- and said:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You look different.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I asked how.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The eye. It is getting lighter. It has already begun.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I touched my face. I felt no difference. But he saw.

\noindent He saw because he has already been through this.

\noindent He saw because he is the mirror.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What happens now?\textquotedblright{}}, I asked.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What happens is that I am going to die.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Die? Why?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Because the creature needs space. She no longer fits inside me. I am old. Weak. She needs a new body. Yours.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}No.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It is already done, Doctor. You read the diary. You dreamed of the trench. You felt the smell. You are turning yellow. It is already done.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I denied it. I said I would commit him. I said I would burn the diary. I said I would stop it.

\noindent He smiled.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Burn it if you like. The story is not on the paper. It is in your head. The creature is not made of ink. It is made of words. And the words are inside you now.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He reached out his hand.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Thank you.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Thank you for what?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}For replacing me. I am tired. 72 years carrying her. Now I can rest.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He closed his eyes.

\noindent And he died.

\noindent In front of me.

\noindent In the session chair.

\noindent The yellow eye went out.

\noindent But, in the same instant, MY right eye lit up.

\noindent I felt a pain. A burn. I ran to the bathroom mirror.

\noindent The right eye is golden.

\noindent Glowing.

\noindent Just like his.

\noindent EYE JUST LIKE HIS.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The diary is in my hand.

\noindent It is warm.

\noindent The pages are moving on their own.

\noindent They are filling with letters. My handwriting. But I am not writing.

\noindent The hand is writing on its own.

\noindent It is writing:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}THE CYCLE BEGINS AGAIN. PATIENT 1,261. HEITOR OLIVEIRA.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent No.
NO.

\noindent IT IS NOT TRUE.

\noindent THIS IS A DELUSION. THIS IS SUGGESTION. THIS IS---

\noindent \textit{im writing with my left hand now. the right one wont stop. it writes on its own. the diary is full of words i didnt write. but they are mine. the handwriting is mine. but it wasnt me.}

\noindent \textit{hes writing through me.}

\noindent \textit{the creature.}

\noindent \textit{shes writing.}

\noindent \textit{the hand.}

\noindent \textit{wont stop.}

\noindent \textit{WONT STOP.}

\noindent \textit{WO---}
