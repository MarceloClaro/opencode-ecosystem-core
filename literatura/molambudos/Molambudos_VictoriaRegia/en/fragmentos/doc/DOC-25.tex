\section*{\textbf{DOC-25} --- The Man in the Black Cloth}
\markboth{DOC-25 --- The Man in the Black Cloth}{}

\noindent \textit{Document extracted from the forensic file.}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent {\footnotesize\texttt{PUBLIC ARCHIVE $\cdot$ DOCUMENTARY EVIDENCE $\cdot$ CASE 1,260}}\\[-0.2em]

\noindent \textbf{Minas Gerais Public Archive --- Informal Testimony}\\
\noindent \textbf{Witness:} Wellington Alves da Silva, 34 years old, property security guard (outsourced).\\
\noindent \textbf{Interviewer:} Dr. Lúcia Mendes (recorded with authorization).\\
\noindent\textbf{Date:} January 15, 2026. \textbf{Location:} Staff break room, MGP Archive.

\noindent \textbf{Lúcia:} Were you at the front desk when the object was returned?

\noindent \textbf{Wellington:} I was. Monday, January 5. Around 6:30 p.m. The front desk was about to close. I was doing the final round.

\noindent \textbf{Lúcia:} Did you see who delivered it?

\noindent \textbf{Wellington:} I did. A man. Hard to say how old. Could have been 50. Could have been 70. His face was... how do I explain... it was a face you forget while you are looking at it. I looked at him and a second later I no longer remembered the nose, the mouth, nothing. Only the eyes. The eyes were the only thing that stayed.

\noindent \textbf{Lúcia:} What color?

\noindent \textbf{Wellington:} (long pause) Have you ever seen a yellow eye? Not a sick person's eye. An animal's eye. An animal that sees in the dark. It was like that. Light yellow, almost golden, with the black pupil in the middle. The pupil was not round. It was... (vertical gesture) ...slit. Like a goat's.

\noindent \textbf{Lúcia:} Did he say anything?

\noindent \textbf{Wellington:} He did. He said: \textquotedblleft{}This is for the doctor.\textquotedblright{} That is all. He put the package on the counter, turned around, and left. The package was a black cloth. One of those thick cloths, you know? For covering saints. When I touched it, it was warm. As if it had been held close to a body for a long time.

\noindent \textbf{Lúcia:} Did you open it?

\noindent \textbf{Wellington:} God forbid. I could tell what it was from the shape. A notebook. An old notebook. But it was not the notebook that scared me. It was the cloth. When I went to fold the cloth to put it away, it was stained. It was not an ink stain. It was a dark thing, sort of brownish, with a smell... (pause) ...you are going to think I am crazy.

\noindent \textbf{Lúcia:} Go ahead.

\noindent \textbf{Wellington:} The cloth smelled of wet undergrowth. But not garden undergrowth. Undergrowth from a place where they buried something. That smell of turned earth. When I breathed in, I tasted rust on my tongue.

\noindent \textbf{Lúcia:} Did you keep the cloth?

\noindent \textbf{Wellington:} I threw it away. That same night. Inside a tied bag. (pause) The next day, the bag had disappeared from the trash bin.

\noindent \textbf{Lúcia:} Disappeared?

\noindent \textbf{Wellington:} The bin was empty. The bag was not. Only the cloth. The cloth came out of the tied bag and disappeared. You can ask Manoel, from the cleaning crew. He saw it. He said nothing like that ever happened. I said: \textquotedblleft{}Now it has.\textquotedblright{}

\noindent \textit{Archivist's note: Wellington Alves da Silva resigned 10 days after this testimony. The resignation form records no reason. His last working day was January 25, 2026 --- 13 days before the diary was removed from the archive for the second time.}
\noindent \textbf{Addendum --- Later identification (Dr. Lúcia Mendes, February 4, 2026):}

\noindent I cross-referenced Wellington's description with the photograph on Dr. Heitor Oliveira's ID badge (1979) and with the photo found inside the diary --- an older man, sitting on a park bench, with a book on his lap.

\noindent The bone structure is the same. The shape of the skull, the angle of the jaw, the distance between the eyes. The man in the black cloth \textbf{is} Heitor Oliveira.

\noindent He would have been 81 in January 2026. The age matches.

\noindent But the slit pupil described by Wellington --- goat's eye, vertical --- is not human. It is not aging. It is transformation. The entity did not kill him. She \textit{kept} him. 47 years of lodging changed his body. The right eye is no longer a human eye. It is a slit. A portal. The diary used Oliveira as a courier --- because he was the only one who could still walk among the living without raising suspicion.

\noindent Patient 1,261 did not disappear. He was \textit{kept} for the moment of the delivery.

\noindent \textit{Final note: Dr. Lúcia Mendes attached this identification to the case file on February 4, 2026. Seven months later, she disappeared. The last person to see her was a bus station guard, who described a woman with a yellow right eye boarding a bus to Ceará.}
