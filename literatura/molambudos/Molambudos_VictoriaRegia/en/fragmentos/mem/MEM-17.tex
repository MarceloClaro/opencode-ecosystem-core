\section*{\textbf{MEM-17} --- The Electroshock}
\markboth{MEM-17 --- The Electroshock}{}

\noindent \textit{The first time}

\noindent \textbf{1937 --- Barbacena Colony}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The first time was in 1937.

\noindent Two orderlies came into the cell. One of them carried a machine. It looked like an old radio, with wires and metal buttons. The other carried a leather strap.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The doctor ordered it\textquotedblright{}, said one.}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}No.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}That's not a question.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent They held me. Two men. I was thin, weak, with no strength. But the creature inside me had strength.

\noindent She woke up.

\noindent I felt the heat rise from my stomach. The rage. The hunger. Her hand pushed my hand. I --- not I --- struck the orderly's face. The bone of his nose cracked.

\noindent The other one hit me with the leather strap.

\noindent I fell.

\noindent They tied me to the bed. Arms stretched out to the sides. Legs together. The leather strap on my head --- tight, cutting into my forehead, pinning my skull against the mattress.

\noindent One of them put something in my mouth. Rubber. So I would not bite my tongue.

\noindent The other turned on the machine.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It will hurt\textquotedblright{}\textit{, he said. }\textquotedblleft{}But it passes.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He was lying.

\noindent It does not pass.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The shock entered through the left side of my head.

\noindent It was not like a fright. It was like a blackout. I was there and I was not. The body trembled. The teeth beat against the rubber. The tongue bled. I heard a moan --- it was mine, but it did not sound like mine. It sounded like an animal.

\noindent The creature screamed inside me.

\noindent Not in pain.
In rage.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I will kill them\textquotedblright{}\textit{, she said. }\textquotedblleft{}I will kill them all.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}No\textquotedblright{}\textit{, I thought. }\textquotedblleft{}No.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent But she did not hear me.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I will kill. I will eat. I will grow inside you until I no longer fit. And then I will come out.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The shock stopped.

\noindent I lay there, panting, with the taste of blood in my mouth and my head pulsing.

\noindent They untied me.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}More tomorrow\textquotedblright{}, they said.}

\noindent And they left.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent There was more.

\noindent Thirty-seven times. Over the course of 15 years. Thirty-seven electroshock sessions, without anesthesia, without sedation. Thirty-seven times that electricity entered my brain and burned pieces of me.

\noindent Every time the shock passed, the creature grew a little more.

\noindent The shock did not affect her.
The shock affected \textit{me}.
She fed on the wreckage.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}They are strengthening me\textquotedblright{}\textit{, she would say. }\textquotedblleft{}Each shock takes a piece of you and gives it to me.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Why?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Because pain is my food. The more you suffer, the more I grow.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Then stop feeling pain.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You cannot.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She was right.

\noindent I never could.

\noindent After the tenth session, I stopped counting. After the twentieth, I stopped feeling. Not because the pain passed --- because I learned to \textit{leave} my body during the shock.

\noindent I would float out. Stay on the ceiling, looking down, watching my body tremble on the bed. The electricity passed. The body shook. And I watched.

\noindent The creature stayed.

\noindent She never left the body.
She took the chance to stretch.
To occupy the spaces the shock opened.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent After 37 sessions, the doctors gave up.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Does not respond to treatment\textquotedblright{}\textit{, they wrote in the record. }\textquotedblleft{}Refractory.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I was not refractory to treatment.
I was refractory to healing.
Because the creature did not want to be healed.
She wanted to grow.

\noindent And she grew.

\noindent Today, looking back, I understand: the Colony was not a hospital.
It was a monster factory.

\noindent And I was their best product.
