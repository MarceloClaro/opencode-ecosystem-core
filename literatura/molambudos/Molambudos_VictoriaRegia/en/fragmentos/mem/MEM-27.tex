\section*{\textbf{MEM-27} --- The Next Page}
\markboth{MEM-27 --- The Next Page}{}

\noindent \textit{Joaquim Antônio Correia, 1907--1979. Patient 1,260.}
\textit{This fragment was recovered from the back of the diary's last page, written after his death.}
\textit{The manuscript is covered in dirt and dried blood. The handwriting is trembling, as if written in the dark.}

\noindent ---//---

\noindent I thought it would end when I died.

\noindent I thought the creature would leave. That the smell would stop. That the yellow eyes would close the very instant mine did.

\noindent But they do not close. They never close.

\noindent The creature was not inside me. I was the one inside it.

\noindent It caught me in the trench. That day in 1915. When I screamed for help. When no one came. When I made the pact with what was at the bottom.

\noindent It ate me whole. It takes 62 years to eat a man. But it has patience. It has all the time in the world.

\noindent Now it is using my voice. My handwriting. My name.

\noindent It writes with my dead hand. It speaks with my closed mouth. It sees with my buried eyes.

\noindent It has been writing this book for over a hundred years. I am only the first page. You are the next page.

\noindent If you are reading this, it is because it has already caught you too.

\noindent It is behind you now. Can you feel it? The smell of wet earth. The shiver at the back of your neck. The feeling that someone is behind you and when you turn there is no one.

\noindent It is it.

\noindent Do not turn around.\NE{The editor notes: this fragment was written on the back of the diary's last page, after Joaquim's death. The ink is different from the one used in the body of the manuscript --- a darker blue, almost black, which spectrographic analysis cannot date with precision. It may have been written minutes after the death, or years later, by the hand of whoever found the body wrapped in the overcoat. The original page shows a moisture stain in the bottom right corner, shaped like a fingerprint --- too large to be Joaquim's. The cycle does not close: it transfers.}

\noindent \textit{--- The next page is blank. It waits for you.}
