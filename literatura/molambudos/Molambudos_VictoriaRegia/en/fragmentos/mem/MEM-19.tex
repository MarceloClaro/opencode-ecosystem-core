\section*{\textbf{MEM-19} --- The Visitor}
\markboth{MEM-19 --- The Visitor}{}

\noindent \textit{Someone came to see me}

\noindent \textbf{1950 --- Someone came to see me}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent No one ever came to see me.

\noindent I had no family. I had no friends. Old Anselmo had died in the corral. My mother had disappeared in the truck. The rest --- neighbors, acquaintances --- had died in the drought or scattered across the world.

\noindent I was a man without a past.

\noindent That is why, when the orderly said \textit{\textquotedblleft{}A visit for you\textquotedblright{}}, I thought it was a mistake.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Who?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I do not know. A woman. She is in the visiting room.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent A woman.

\noindent My mother? Impossible. She would be over 70. And she was dead --- or worse.

\noindent But I went.

\noindent I crossed the corridor. The other patients stared. Visits were rare at the Colony. People did not visit the mad. The mad were forgotten.

\noindent The visiting room was a room with two chairs and a table. On one of the chairs, a woman sat.

\noindent She was elderly. White hair, wrinkled skin, hands calloused from work. She wore a simple blue dress with a shawl over her shoulders.

\noindent She raised her eyes when I came in.

\noindent Her eyes were brown. Tired. But alive.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Joaquim?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The voice was trembling.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}...Who are you?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She smiled. A sad smile.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I am Maria. Maria do Rosário.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent My mother.

\noindent I sat down.

\noindent I kept looking at her. Trying to find the woman who led me by the hand on the road out of Senador Pompeu. The woman who coughed in the corral. The woman who was taken away in the truck.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You...\textquotedblright{}\textit{ My voice failed. }\textquotedblleft{}You are alive.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I am.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Where were you?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She lowered her eyes.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I was taken to a brothel in Fortaleza. I stayed there for 3 years. Then I fled. I married again. I had other children. I lived in São Paulo.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And you never...\textquotedblright{}\textit{ I swallowed. }\textquotedblleft{}Never came back?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I did.\textquotedblright{}\textit{ She looked at me. }\textquotedblleft{}I came back to get you. In 1919. They told me you had died.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}A lie.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I know. I found out last year. A neighbor said her son worked here and that there was a patient from Ceará. 1,260. The same name. I came to see.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Did you come to take me away?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She did not answer.

\noindent She looked at her hands.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Joaquim, I...\textquotedblright{}\textit{ She stopped. }\textquotedblleft{}I have another family. Other children. They do not know about you. They do not know I had a son before.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And will you tell them?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She did not answer.

\noindent I understood.

\noindent She had not come to take me away.
She had come to \textit{say goodbye}.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Okay\textquotedblright{}\textit{, I said. }\textquotedblleft{}It is all right.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Joaquim...\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It is all right, Mother.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The word \textit{mother} came out without my thinking. She heard it. Her eyes filled with tears.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Forgive me\textquotedblright{}}, she said.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}For what?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}For leaving you.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You did not leave. They took you.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}But I did not come back.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You came back now.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She wept.

\noindent I did not weep. The creature inside me would not let me.

\noindent She stayed for 20 more minutes. She told me about her life. The other children. The husband. The house in São Paulo. I listened. I nodded. I smiled in the right places.

\noindent When she left, she hugged me.

\noindent Her hug was warm. Soft. It smelled of soap.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I will write to you\textquotedblright{}}, she said.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Okay.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She did not write.

\noindent Not ever again.

\noindent But that night, the creature stayed quiet. She respected the silence.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Are you sad?\textquotedblright{}} she asked.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}No.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Yes, you are.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}She is not coming back.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I know.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}But why did she come?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I thought.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}So I would know she existed. So I would know I was not a dream.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The creature did not answer.

\noindent That night, for the first time in years, I did not feel hunger.

\noindent I felt longing.

\noindent Longing for a mother I barely knew.
Longing for a life I never had.
Longing to be someone's son.

\noindent Longing to be human.
