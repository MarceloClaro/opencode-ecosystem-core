\section*{\textbf{MEM-22} --- The Voices}
\markboth{MEM-22 --- The Voices}{}

\noindent \textit{The ward that screams}

\noindent \textbf{1952 --- The ward that screams}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The ward of the agitated stood next to the ward of the silenced.

\noindent Two wards. Two worlds. On one side, people who would not stop screaming. On the other, people who could no longer speak.

\noindent I stayed in the middle.

\noindent During the day, I heard the screams. Screams of fear. Screams of rage. Screams without meaning. Screams that were only sound, without words, without significance.

\noindent At night, the silence of the silenced was worse. Because silence screams too. Only silence screams inside your head.

\noindent The creature liked the screams.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Listen\textquotedblright{}}\textit{, she said. }\textit{\textquotedblleft{}Every scream is a soul coming loose. Every scream is a piece of someone leaving. When the scream stops, the person is already gone.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Listen\textquotedblright{}\textit{, she would say. }\textquotedblleft{}Each scream is a soul coming loose. Each scream is a piece of someone going away. When the scream stops, the person is already gone.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Gone where?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Inside me.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You eat the screams?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I eat everything. I eat the pain. I eat the fear. I eat the madness. It is all food.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I covered my ears.

\noindent But the creature heard for me.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent One night, a different scream.

\noindent It was not fear. It was loss. Someone screaming someone's name. A woman. She screamed: \textit{\textquotedblleft{}José! José!\textquotedblright{}} The name echoed down the corridor for minutes.

\noindent Then, silence.

\noindent The next morning, I asked the orderly who José was.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}He died yesterday. Pneumonia.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And who was screaming?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}His wife. They admitted them together in 1940. He died. She stayed.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The woman never screamed again.

\noindent She stayed silent for the rest of her life.

\noindent Three months later, she too died.

\noindent They say it was malnutrition.

\noindent I think it was having no one left to scream for.
