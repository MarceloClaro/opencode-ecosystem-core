\section*{\textbf{MEM-18} --- The Escape Attempt}
\markboth{MEM-18 --- The Escape Attempt}{}

\noindent \textit{1943 --- the year I almost made it}

\noindent \textbf{1943 --- The year I almost made it}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent I planned the escape for two years.

\noindent 1941 to 1943. Day after day, watching the routine. The orderly who arrived late on Mondays. The pantry window that never closed properly. The hole in the back fence, hidden by the brush.

\noindent The creature helped me.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You will make it\textquotedblright{}\textit{, she said. }\textquotedblleft{}You will get out of here. You will see the world. You will feel the sun again.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Why do you want me to escape?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Because in here you are small. Out there you can be great. Out there there are more people. More food. More... room.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I should have been suspicious.

\noindent The creature never wanted anything good for me.
She always wanted what was good for \textit{her}.

\noindent But I was young. 1943, I was 36. Twenty-six years spent inside the Colony. I wanted to feel the wind on my face with no bars in between.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent On the night of the escape, the sky was clear. The stars shone like never before. I waited for the orderly to make the 2 a.m. round, counted to a thousand, and got up.

\noindent The cell door was not locked --- I had learned to open it with a sliver of metal I hid in the sole of my foot. I went down the corridor. Silence. The pantry. The window. Open.

\noindent I jumped.

\noindent The brush was tall. The ground was cold. I ran. My bare feet beating the ground. The hole in the fence. I went through.

\noindent I was \textit{out}.

\noindent The world was big. Dark. Silent. But it was open. I looked at the sky and saw the stars with no bars in front of them.

\noindent I cried.

\noindent Not from sadness.
From joy.

\noindent After 26 years, I was free.

\noindent For 15 minutes.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The creature spoke while I ran down the dirt road.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Where are you going?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Far.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Far where?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Bahia. They say there is work there.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And what will you do?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Work. Eat. Live.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Live?\textquotedblright{}\textit{ She laughed. }\textquotedblleft{}You do not know how to live. You only know how to survive.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I will learn.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You will not. You are an asylum animal. You do not know how to talk to people. You do not know how to look into eyes without frightening. You do not know what normal is.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Shut up.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And your eye? Do you think anyone will hire a man with a yellow eye that glows in the dark?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I stopped running.

\noindent I stood still in the middle of the road, panting.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Shut up.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You have nowhere to go. The world out there does not want an ex-patient of the Colony. The world wants normal people. You are not normal. You are Patient 1,260. That is what is written on you.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I will tear that number off my skin.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The number is not on your skin, Joaquim. It is in your soul. You can tear off your whole skin, and the 1,260 will still be there.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I sat down in the middle of the road.

\noindent The dust rose around me.

\noindent The stars kept shining, beautiful, distant.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The orderlies found me sitting in the same spot, 6 hours later.

\noindent I had not run.
I had not hidden.
I just stayed there, waiting.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What happened?\textquotedblright{}\textit{, asked the orderly. }\textquotedblleft{}Gave up?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}No.\textquotedblright{}\textit{ I stood up. }\textquotedblleft{}I just found out there is nowhere to go.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent They took me back.

\noindent The cell was the same. The concrete bed. The bucket. The bars. All the same.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Welcome back\textquotedblright{}, said the creature.}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Shut up.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She laughed.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Next time, Joaquim, you do not try to escape. You stay. You wait. You write. One day the diary gets out. And then...\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Then what?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Then the escape happens. But it is not you who leaves. It is the diary. The escape is its.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And me?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You stay. You always stay. The diary is the one that will see the world.\textquotedblright{}}\NE{The escape passage is the most cohesive and linear in all of Joaquim's diary --- conventional narrative structure, with a beginning, middle, and end, which contrasts with the fragmentation of the other fragments. This cohesion suggests later rewriting, or the hand of the entity organizing the narrative for the reader who would find it.}

\noindent She was right.

\noindent I never tried to escape again.
But the diary did.

\noindent The diary escaped for me.
