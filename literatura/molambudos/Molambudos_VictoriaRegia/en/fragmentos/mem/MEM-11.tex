\section*{\textbf{MEM-11} --- The Isolation}
\markboth{MEM-11 --- The Isolation}{}

\noindent \textit{The ward of the silenced}

\noindent \textbf{1923-1945 --- The ward of the silenced}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent After the second homicide, they moved me to the ward of the silenced.

\noindent It was the end of the corridor. A ward that did not appear in the official records. The patients kept there did not exist for the hospital. They were bodies the hospital kept alive --- barely alive --- so as not to have to explain too many deaths.

\noindent The ward had twelve cells. Six on each side. The cells were small --- two square meters. Concrete bed. No mattress. No blanket. A hole in the floor for necessities.

\noindent The patients arrived and never left.

\noindent I saw men enter the ward at forty and die at forty-five. Shrunken. Gray-haired. With rotten teeth and skin full of sores.

\noindent We did not speak. Not because we did not want to --- because we forgot how. Silence became a disease. First the voice disappears. Then the words. Then thought.

\noindent The creature was the only one who spoke.

\noindent She filled the silence.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You will not become like them\textquotedblright{}, she said.}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Like what?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Empty.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I am already empty.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}No. You have the diary. You write. As long as you write, you exist. They ---\textquotedblright{}\textit{ she pointed at the other patients }\textquotedblleft{}--- they stopped writing. Stopped speaking. Stopped existing.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}They are alive.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Living is not existing. Living is the body. Existing is the story. If no one remembers you, you do not exist.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Will anyone remember?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I will.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She fell quiet.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And whoever reads the diary.\textquotedblright{}}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent On winter nights, the cold came in through the cracks in the wall and would not leave. I slept curled up, with the diary on my chest, warming it with my body.

\noindent The creature stayed quiet on those nights.

\noindent But I felt her watching.

\noindent Waiting.

\noindent She knew I was going to die there. She knew the diary would be buried with me. And she knew that, one day, someone would open it.

\noindent The diary was her egg.

\noindent And I was the nest.
