\section*{\textbf{MEM-05} --- The Yellow Eye}
\markboth{MEM-05 --- The Yellow Eye}{}

\noindent \textit{The one that never closed again}

\noindent \textbf{The one that never closed again}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The next morning, my mother looked at me and said:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Joaquim, your eye.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I touched my face. My right eye was wide open. I could not blink. The eyelid was stiff, as if it had been sewn open.

\noindent I ran to the water jar and leaned over. The water was dirty, muddy, but I could see the reflection.

\noindent The iris of my right eye was yellow.

\noindent Not the yellow of jaundice, which turns the whole eye egg-yolk colored. It was an intense, bright yellow, with a black pupil that seemed deeper than it should be. As if inside my eye there were a well.

\noindent My mother crossed herself.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What did you see?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I did not answer.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Joaquim, for the love of God, what did you see in that trench?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Nothing\textquotedblright{}\textit{, I lied. }\textquotedblleft{}I didn't see anything.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She knew it was a lie. But she did not ask again. That day, the soldiers came for the women. They said they were going to \textquotedblleft{}relocate\textquotedblright{} the girls to the coast. My mother was coughing, coughing, and even so they took her.

\noindent She looked at me as the truck drove away.

\noindent Her eye said: \textit{don't die.}

\noindent I did not die.

\noindent But I wished I had.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent That night, I went back to the trench.

\noindent The creature was there. This time, she was not eating. She was lying among the bodies, looking at the sky. When she saw me, she sat up.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I knew you would come back.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I stayed silent.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Come here.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I went. This time, I did not sit at the edge. I climbed down. My feet sank into the pile of bodies. Some were soft. Others hard. One had its arm stretched out, pointing at nothing. I stepped on his hand by accident and it broke with a dry sound.

\noindent Up close, the creature had a different smell. It was not just meat. It was thick blood, wet earth, and something sweet that reminded me of the smell of a cattle pen.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What's your name?\textquotedblright{}}, she asked.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Joaquim.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Joaquim.\textquotedblright{}\textit{ She repeated it as if tasting the word. }\textquotedblleft{}A saint's name. Joseph, Joachim, Mary. A name for someone born to suffer.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And yours?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She laughed. The laugh was a wet sound, like water passing through dirty pipes.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I have no name. But I have hunger. And hunger needs a name to be called.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Call it Eye.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She tilted her head.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Why?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Because it's the first part of you I ever saw.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She smiled. Her teeth shone in the dark.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Then I shall be Eye. For you.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She reached out her hand. The enormous hand, with too many fingers, and touched my face.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Your eye is now like mine\textquotedblright{}\textit{, she said. }\textquotedblleft{}You see what I see.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And what do you see?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I see hunger.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Inside whom?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Inside everyone. But most do not know. You will know. You will see in every face what it hides. You will see the hole each one carries inside. And you will know what fills that hole.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She tilted her head to the side, like a bird.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Me.\textquotedblright{}}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent On the third night, the creature showed me what I was going to become.

\noindent She touched my forehead with her finger --- a long finger, with a black nail --- and I \textit{saw}.

\noindent I saw a ward. A large ward, full of beds. Men lying down. Men screaming. Men who did not move. I saw a boy --- older than me, but still a boy --- sitting in a corner. The boy moved his mouth but no sound came out. He was writing in a notebook. The handwriting was mine.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}That is you\textquotedblright{}\textit{, said the creature. }\textquotedblleft{}In a few years.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Where is that?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}A place that keeps people like me. People society buried alive. You will stay there a long time.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And you?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I go with you.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She touched my chest. Her finger passed through the skin, the bones --- it did not hurt --- and touched something inside me.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I am already here. I will not leave again.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And if I don't want you?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She laughed again.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You have no choice, Joaquim. The night you sat at the edge of my trench, you invited me. Not with words. With hunger. Your hunger called me. And I came.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I can leave.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You can. But I will be inside you.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And if I die?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She fell quiet. For the first time, her silence was different. It was not threatening. It was... sad.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}If you die, I wait.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Wait for what?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Until someone remembers you. And opens what you wrote. And reads.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And then?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Her yellow eye shone in the dark.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Then I go again.\textquotedblright{}}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent When I raised my head, an owl perched on a dead tree was watching me.

\noindent It had made no sound. It was there, motionless, watching me with black lidless eyes. Its head turned slowly --- a hundred and eighty degrees --- and it fixed its gaze on the creature.

\noindent The creature did not look up. But she smiled.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}She came to meet you.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Who is she?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}She is my tongue in the world above. Whatever you say, she carries. Whatever she sees, I know.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The owl spread its wings and flew. The flight was silent, absorbed by the night as if the dark were wet cloth.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Will she follow me?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}No. She goes ahead. Preparing the way.\textquotedblright{}}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent I am 72 years old. I am writing this in a psychiatric hospital ward. The doctors say I am mad. They say the creature is a product of my mind. That the yellow eye is a badly healed cataract.

\noindent I know it is not.

\noindent She is here now. While I write. I feel her weight on the back of my neck. The sweet breath. She is reading what I write. And she is laughing.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Tell him\textquotedblright{}\textit{, she whispers. }\textquotedblleft{}Tell him the diary does not end when you die.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I will die soon. I know it. Dr. Oliveira said my heart cannot take much more.

\noindent But the diary will go on.

\noindent It is not I who writes.

\noindent It is she.

\noindent \textit{--- Joaquim Antônio Correia, Patient 1,260. Last entry.}
