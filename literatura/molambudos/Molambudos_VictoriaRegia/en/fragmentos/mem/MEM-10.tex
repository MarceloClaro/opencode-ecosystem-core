\section*{\textbf{MEM-10} --- The First Homicide}
\markboth{MEM-10 --- The First Homicide}{}

\noindent \textit{What hunger teaches}

\noindent \textbf{1922 --- Barbacena Colony}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent I was fifteen years old.

\noindent The orderly's name was Wagner. He was big, fat, with thick arms and short fingers. He wore a white apron that was never clean. He liked to hit.

\noindent He did not hit out of anger. He hit for fun. He walked through the corridors and picked one. \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Today it's you.\textquotedblright{}} And he hit. With an open hand. With a belt. With the hose rubber he kept in his pocket.

\noindent The patients learned to shrink. To make themselves small. To not look into his eyes.

\noindent I did not shrink.

\noindent Wagner did not like that.

\noindent One day he came to me. I was in the yard, sitting on the ground, writing in the diary. He kicked the diary out of my hand. The pages flew.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What do you write there, boy?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Nothing.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Nothing, what?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Nothing, sir.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He grabbed me by the neck. Lifted me up. His hand tightened. The fingers sank into the flesh.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Are you crazy?\textquotedblright{}\textit{ he asked. }\textquotedblleft{}You know I can kill you right here?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I knew.

\noindent I did not answer.

\noindent He squeezed harder.

\noindent That was when the creature spoke.

\noindent Not inside my head. Outside. The voice came out of my mouth. But it was not my voice.

\noindent It was the voice of the trench.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Take your hand off.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Wagner froze.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Take your hand off.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The voice was not loud. It was low. Thick. As if it came from a well.

\noindent Wagner looked into my eyes. The yellow eye. He saw.

\noindent He saw the creature inside me.

\noindent He opened his hand.

\noindent I fell to the ground.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You...\textquotedblright{}} he began.

\noindent He did not finish.

\noindent I do not remember jumping. I only remember being on top of him. My hand on his face. My fingers in his eyes. He screamed.

\noindent I did not stop.

\noindent Wagner had a rubber hose in his pocket. I took it. I hit. I hit. I hit.

\noindent I do not remember how many times.

\noindent When the other orderlies pulled me off him, Wagner's face was no longer a face. It was a red paste.

\noindent The rubber lay on the ground, dirty with blood and eye.

\noindent His eye was open, on the ground, looking at me.

\noindent I picked up the diary. Hid it under my shirt.

\noindent That was when I saw the boy.

\noindent A new patient, skinny, about twelve years old. He was curled up in the corner of the yard, hands on his head, trembling. An orderly approached him. Not the one I had killed --- another. Bigger. With an iron key in his hand.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}This one goes to isolation too\textquotedblright{}\textit{, he said. }\textquotedblleft{}Witness.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The boy began to cry.

\noindent And I heard the creature inside me. She said:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Let it go. Not your problem.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent But I walked.

\noindent I placed myself in front of the boy.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}He didn't see anything\textquotedblright{}\textit{, I said. }\textquotedblleft{}He had his back turned the whole time.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The orderly looked at me. The iron key in his hand was heavy. I watched him think.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And who guarantees it?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I do\textquotedblright{}\textit{, I said. }\textquotedblleft{}I'm the madman who killed a man. What is my word worth?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He spat on the ground.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Nothing.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent But he lowered the key. And he walked away.

\noindent The boy stayed. Alive. Whole. Because of me.

\noindent The creature inside me was quiet. She did not understand what I had done. Neither did I.

\noindent But, for the first time, the choice had been mine.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}He started it\textquotedblright{}, I said.}

\noindent They took me to isolation.

\noindent I spent three months in a cell without a window. Without light. Without proper food. Without anyone.

\noindent The creature stayed quiet inside me. Satisfied.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Did you like it?\textquotedblright{}, I asked.}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You liked it too.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She was right.

\noindent I felt no remorse. I felt no fear. I felt something I did not know.

\noindent Power.

\noindent For the first time in my life, I was not hungry.

\noindent I had killed the hunger.

\noindent With blood.
