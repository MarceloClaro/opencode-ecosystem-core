\section*{\textbf{MEM-08} --- Arrival at the Colony}
\markboth{MEM-08 --- Arrival at the Colony}{}

\noindent \textit{1917 --- Barbacena, Minas Gerais}

\noindent \textbf{1917 --- Barbacena, Minas Gerais}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent I do not remember how I arrived at the Colony.

\noindent I remember the truck. I remember sacks. I remember it was dark and that there were many of us in a small space. People who smelled of sweat, of feces, of fear.

\noindent Then the truck stopped.

\noindent I saw the gate.

\noindent It was an iron gate, black, with points on top. Behind the gate there was a dirt road. Beyond the road there was a building. The building was large, gray, with small windows and a cross on its peak.

\noindent A hospital cross. But it looked like a cemetery cross.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Get down\textquotedblright{}}, someone said.

\noindent I got down.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Name?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Joaquim Antônio Correia.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Age?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Ten.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The man looked at me. Looked at my yellow eye. Wrote something on a paper.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Diagnosis?\textquotedblright{}} he asked another man.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Came from Ceará. Government pen. Orphan. Doesn't speak.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I do speak\textquotedblright{}}, I said.

\noindent The two men looked at me.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Then speak\textquotedblright{}\textit{, said the first. }\textquotedblleft{}What's wrong with you?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I thought about telling the truth. I thought about telling them about the trench. The creature. The eye. The hunger that never passes.

\noindent But I had learned something on the road and in the pen: the truth does not help.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Nothing\textquotedblright{}\textit{, I said. }\textquotedblleft{}There's nothing wrong with me.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The man laughed.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Everyone who arrives here has something wrong. If you don't, we invent it.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He wrote on the paper:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Patient 1,260 --- Joaquim Antônio Correia. Hebephrenic schizophrenia. Dangerous. Isolation.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I did not know what schizophrenia was. I learned later. It is a word doctors use when they do not know what a person has.

\noindent They took me to a room.

\noindent The room had an iron bed. A barred window. A bucket in the corner.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Stay here\textquotedblright{}\textit{, said the orderly. }\textquotedblleft{}The doctor comes tomorrow.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And the food?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Tomorrow.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He closed the door. The key turned.

\noindent I stayed there, sitting on the bed, looking at the wall. The wall was full of scratches. Fingernail scratches. Teeth scratches. Scratches from people who had been there before me.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Welcome\textquotedblright{}}, said the creature inside me.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Shut up.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I will like it here\textquotedblright{}\textit{, she said. }\textquotedblleft{}There is much hunger here.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She was right.

\noindent The Colony had more than five thousand patients. The dormitories were sheds with beds touching one another. The food was a bone broth with flour. Water was served once a day.

\noindent The screams never stopped.

\noindent At night, the screams echoed through the corridors. Screams of pain. Screams of fear. Screams of people who no longer knew who they were.

\noindent In the first days, I covered my ears.

\noindent After a week, I was screaming along with them.

\noindent The creature grew.

\noindent Every scream fed her. Every tear. Every piece of food I swallowed that did not satisfy.

\noindent She fed on the Colony.

\noindent And the Colony fed on me.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent Years later, Dr. Oliveira asked me:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Joaquim, do you know how many years you spent in the Colony?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I knew.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Sixty-two.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And how do you feel about that?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I looked at him. The yellow eye shone.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I didn't live here, Doctor. I rotted here.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He took notes.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}To rot\textquotedblright{}\textit{, he repeated. }\textquotedblleft{}Interesting choice of words.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It wasn't a choice\textquotedblright{}\textit{, I said. }\textquotedblleft{}It's what happened. People don't choose to rot. People just... rot.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He stopped writing.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What happened to you, Joaquim?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I smiled.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What didn't?\textquotedblright{}}
