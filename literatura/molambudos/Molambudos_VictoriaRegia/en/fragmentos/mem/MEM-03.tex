\section*{\textbf{MEM-03} --- The Father's Death}
\markboth{MEM-03 --- The Father's Death}{}

\noindent \textit{The second day without water}

\noindent \textbf{1915 --- The second day without water}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent My father sat down beneath a juazeiro tree.

\noindent The tree was dead --- no leaves, no shade. But he sat down. He leaned his back against the trunk and closed his eyes.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Father, let's go.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You go on.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent My mother knelt in front of him.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Antônio, get up.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I can't.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You can. You always said you could.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He opened his eyes. Looked at her. His gaze was far away. As if he were already somewhere else.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Maria, I can't feel my legs.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She looked at his legs. They were swollen. His feet were cracked, with dried blood on the heels.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It's just exhaustion\textquotedblright{}}, she said.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It's death.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Don't say that.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Maria.\textquotedblright{}\textit{ He placed his hand on her face. His hand was nothing but bone. }\textquotedblleft{}Take the boy. Go. Don't stop.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I'm not leaving you here.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I'm already dead. Only the body hasn't stopped yet.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent My mother began to cry. It was the first time I ever saw her cry. Her crying was dry --- there were no tears. Her body had no water for tears.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Joaquim\textquotedblright{}\textit{, my father said. }\textquotedblleft{}Come here.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I went.

\noindent He placed his hand on my head.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You're a man now. You take care of your mother.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Yes, Father.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Don't let her starve.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}...Yes, Father.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You eat whatever there is to eat. Even if...\textquotedblright{}\textit{ He stopped. Looked at the horizon. }\textquotedblleft{}Even if it doesn't look like food.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Yes, Father.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He pulled my face close to his. His breath smelled of an empty stomach.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You will not die of hunger, Joaquim. You hear me? You will not die of hunger. You do whatever must be done. But you will not die of hunger.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Yes, Father.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He let go of my face. Closed his eyes.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You may go.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent My mother stood still.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You may go\textquotedblright{}\textit{, he repeated. }\textquotedblleft{}I said so.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She stood up. Took my hand.

\noindent We walked.

\noindent When I looked back, he was still sitting at the dead juazeiro. Head fallen. Arm stretched out on his lap.

\noindent My mother pulled my hand. \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Come on.\textquotedblright{}} I could have turned my face away. She did not look. But I chose to keep that image. The last one.

\noindent The last time I saw my father, he looked like a saint.

\noindent A saint who died of hunger.
