\section*{\textbf{MEM-25} --- The Death in the Mirror}
\markboth{MEM-25 --- The Death in the Mirror}{}

\noindent \textit{The first time I saw myself old}

\noindent \textbf{1970 --- The first time I saw myself old}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent There was a mirror in the ward of the silenced.

\noindent I do not know why. Someone hung it on the corridor wall and forgot about it. No one used it. Patients do not look in mirrors. Patients do not want to see themselves.

\noindent But one day, I passed in front of it and looked without meaning to.

\noindent The man in the mirror was old.

\noindent White hair. Wrinkled skin. Hollow eyes. The right one yellow, the left one dim.

\noindent A crooked mouth. A stubbled beard. Rotten teeth.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Who is that?\textquotedblright{}}, I thought.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It is you\textquotedblright{}, said the creature.}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}No. I am 8 years old. I am in the Sertão. I am walking with my mother.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You are 63 years old. You are at the Colony. You are alone.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I touched the face in the mirror.

\noindent The wrinkled skin touched my hand.

\noindent The creature said:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Time passed, Joaquim. You did not see it. But it passed.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}How much time?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}63 years.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}...I spent 63 years here?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Yes.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}...And what did I do?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You survived.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}That is all?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}That is all.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I sat on the corridor floor, in front of the mirror.

\noindent I kept looking at the old man who was me.

\noindent I did not remember growing old.

\noindent Time had passed inside me without my noticing.

\noindent The days were the same. The nights were the same. The seasons changed, but the Colony was always the same temperature: cold.

\noindent I had not aged.
I had \textit{rotted}.

\noindent And the worst: no one saw.

\noindent No one noticed.

\noindent Because no one looked at me.

\noindent I was Patient 1,260.
I was a number.
Numbers do not grow old.
Numbers only expire.
