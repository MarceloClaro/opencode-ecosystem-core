\section*{\textbf{MEM-15} --- The Entity Speaks}
\markboth{MEM-15 --- The Entity Speaks}{}

\noindent \textit{The voice that was not Joaquim's}

\noindent \textbf{1979 --- Third session with Dr. Heitor Oliveira}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent Dr. Oliveira arrived different that day.

\noindent He was pale. His eyes red. Unshaven. He sat in the chair and did not open his notepad.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Joaquim, I read the diary.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I know.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The last pages...\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He stopped. He ran his hand over his face.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The last pages were written after your death.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I know.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}How? How could you have written after dying?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I did not answer. The creature inside me began to wake. I felt the heat rising from my stomach.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Joaquim?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The voice that came out was not mine.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}He did not write it.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The doctor straightened his body.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Who... who is speaking?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You already know who.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Dr. Oliveira looked around. He looked at the door. He looked at the window. Everything was closed.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You are... inside him?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Inside. Outside. All around.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What are you?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The creature laughed. My mouth opened. The sound that came out was not human.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I am what was left. I am what was forgotten. I am the hunger that never goes away. I am the trench.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The trench...\textquotedblright{}} repeated Oliveira.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The trench where the bodies were thrown. The trench where no one prayed. The trench where the government threw the molambudos like garbage. I was born there. I grew there. I ate the dead until they gave birth to me.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You are an... entity?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}That. A pretty word. But I am not an entity. I am an accumulation. I am what happens when you throw too many people into the same hole. The earth cannot absorb so much death. It... ferments. It becomes something new.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And Joaquim?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Joaquim is my first child.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Child?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}He came to me. Alone. Hungry. He sat on the edge of the trench and looked at me. I looked back. He did not run. He stayed. He is mine.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}He killed people.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Yes.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Because of you?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}No. Because of hunger. The hunger that humanity created. I only use the hunger that already exists.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Dr. Oliveira fell silent.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What do you want?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Nothing.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Nothing?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I want nothing. I need nothing. I only... exist. I exist between the pages of the diary. I exist in his eye. I exist in the memory of whoever reads.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And what happens when someone reads?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You have already read. Tell me.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Dr. Oliveira did not answer.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You dreamed of me last night\textquotedblright{}\textit{, said the creature. }\textquotedblleft{}You dreamed of the trench. You felt the smell. You saw the eyes. You woke up hungry. A hunger that breakfast did not satisfy.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It was only a dream.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You think so.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The creature laughed again.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Let me ask you a question, Doctor.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}...Go ahead.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Are you afraid to sleep tonight?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The silence was the answer.

\noindent The creature smiled with my mouth.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Then the Cycle has already begun.\textquotedblright{}}
