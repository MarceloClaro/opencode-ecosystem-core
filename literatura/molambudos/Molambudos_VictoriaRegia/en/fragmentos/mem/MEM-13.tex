\section*{\textbf{MEM-13} --- The New Doctor}
\markboth{MEM-13 --- The New Doctor}{}

\noindent \textit{First session with Dr. Heitor Oliveira}

\noindent \textbf{1979 --- First session with Dr. Heitor Oliveira}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent I knew he was new by the way he walked.

\noindent Old doctors walk slowly. They have seen everything. They are in no hurry. New ones walk fast. They think they are going to discover something no one else has discovered.

\noindent Dr. Oliveira walked fast.

\noindent He came into the session room --- a room with two chairs, a table, a barred window --- and sat in front of me. He put a notepad on the table. A pen. He adjusted his white coat.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Good morning, Joaquim.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Good morning.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He looked at me. I looked at him.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I am Dr. Heitor Oliveira. I am the new psychiatrist of the ward.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I know.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}How do you know?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The smell.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He stopped.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Smell?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}A new doctor smells of soap. An old doctor smells of mothballs.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He laughed. It was a nervous laugh.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You are observant.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Sixty-two years in here. You learn.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He wrote something down.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Do you know why you are here, Joaquim?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Because I killed three people.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Why did you kill them?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Because they were in the wrong place at the wrong time.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And why were they in the wrong place?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Because I decided it was the time.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He stopped writing.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Did you plan the murders?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I did not need to. The opportunity appeared. I took it.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Do you feel remorse?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I looked at him. With the yellow eye.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Have you ever been hungry, Doctor?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Yes.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Really hungry? Hungry enough to eat dirt, to eat your own hand, to look at the other person and think 'he has flesh'?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He did not answer.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}When you are hungry like that, there is no room left for remorse. Remorse is a luxury for those with a full stomach.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Are you saying you killed out of hunger?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I am saying hunger killed for me. I was only the tool.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He wrote. His pen wrote fast.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And is that hunger still here?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It never left.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What do you feel now?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I closed my eyes.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Do you want the truth?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I do.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I can smell you.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He stopped writing.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What smell?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You ate meat at dinner. Beef. You put sauce on the meat. It had onion. You drank wine after. Red wine. You brushed your teeth, but the smell is still in the pores of your skin.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He swallowed hard.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What else?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You are afraid. You are afraid right now. You are sitting in a chair trying to look professional, but you want to get up and leave. You heard stories about me. You think I am a monster.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Are you?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I opened my eyes.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Everyone is, Doctor. The difference is that I stopped hiding it.\textquotedblright{}}
