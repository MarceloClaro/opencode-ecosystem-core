\section*{\textbf{MEM-20} --- The Death of My Cell Neighbor}
\markboth{MEM-20 --- The Death of My Cell Neighbor}{}

\noindent \textit{The only friend I ever had}

\noindent \textbf{1963 --- The only friend I ever had}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent His name was Severino.

\noindent From Pará. 52 years old. Committed in 1935 because his family no longer wanted to care for him. Diagnosis: \textquotedblleft{}idiocy\textquotedblright{}. The real diagnosis: a stutter and mild epilepsy. But in the 1930s, a stutter was treated with electroshock. After 30 shocks, Severino no longer stuttered. He no longer spoke either. Nor moved.

\noindent He was in the cell next to mine.

\noindent For 20 years, we talked by knocking on the wall.

\noindent Code:
1 knock = yes
2 knocks = no
3 knocks = how are you
4 knocks = I am hungry
5 knocks = I am afraid

\noindent We developed a language. Long knocks, short knocks. You could say complex sentences. \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The orderly came today\textquotedblright{}} --- 3 long, 2 short, pause, 4 short. I understood.

\noindent Severino was the only person at the Colony who treated me like a human being.

\noindent He did not look at my yellow eye with fear. He looked at me the way you look at a friend.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent In 1963, Severino caught pneumonia.

\noindent The Colony did not treat pneumonia. It let people die. Thirty patients a month died of pneumonia, tuberculosis, malnutrition. The Colony called it \textquotedblleft{}natural selection\textquotedblright{}.

\noindent I heard his cough through the wall.

\noindent The cough lasted a week. Weaker every day. On the seventh day, the cough stopped.

\noindent I knocked on the wall.

\noindent 1 knock. \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Severino?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Silence.

\noindent 2 knocks. \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Wake up.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Silence.

\noindent 3 knocks. \textit{\textquotedblleft{}How are you?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Silence.

\noindent I knew he was dead. But I kept knocking. I knocked all night. Different codes. Different questions. \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Are you there?\textquotedblright{}\textit{ }\textquotedblleft{}Answer me.\textquotedblright{}\textit{ }\textquotedblleft{}Do not leave me alone.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent No one answered.

\noindent In the morning, the orderlies came to take the body.

\noindent I heard the cell door open. I heard the dragging of feet on the floor. Then, silence.

\noindent The cell next door stayed empty.

\noindent For three months, no one occupied it.

\noindent Every now and then I knocked on the wall. Not to talk --- to hear the sound of the hollow.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent After Severino died, the creature spoke:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You will never have a friend again.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Shut up.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It is true. You are old. A lecher. No one wants to be friends with an old lecher.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Shut up.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Severino was the only one. And he died. Because everyone who gets close to you dies.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Shut up!\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I hit the wall with my hand. The hand bled.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It was not my fault.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It was.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It was not.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Severino caught pneumonia last winter. Do you know what caused the pneumonia? The cold. Do you know where the cold came in? Through the cracks in the wall. The wall that separates his cell from yours. The cracks are on his side. But the cold came from your cell.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}A lie.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The truth. You breathed the cold air, the air passed through the wall, it reached him. You killed him without meaning to. But you killed him.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I did not answer.

\noindent I stayed curled in the corner, my hand bleeding, my heart tight.

\noindent She was lying.
She always lied.

\noindent But that night, I felt the cold coming in through the cracks.

\noindent And I thought: \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What if it is true?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent But there, that night, I made a choice. Not the choice to believe her or myself --- the choice to remember Severino as he was: the only friend I ever had. The creature could lie about the cold, about the pneumonia, about the wall. I could choose not to listen. I could choose what to keep of him. And I kept the knocking on the wall. The 5 knocks. The language we built together. That, she could not steal.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent I never made friends with anyone again.

\noindent It was not worth it.
Everyone who got close to me --- the creature found a way to destroy.

\noindent Loneliness was not my choice.
It was the poison she spread around me.

\noindent So that I would depend only on her.
So that I would have no one else.

\noindent She wanted to be my only company.

\noindent And she succeeded.
