\section*{\textbf{MEM-16} --- The Death at the Colony}
\markboth{MEM-16 --- The Death at the Colony}{}

\noindent \textit{The last session}

\noindent \textbf{June 1979 --- The last session}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent Dr. Oliveira arrived at the session room with the diary in his hand. The pages were marked. Some pages were folded. He had read everything.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Joaquim\textquotedblright{}\textit{, he said. }\textquotedblleft{}I need to understand one thing.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Ask.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The creature. Is she here right now?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}She is.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Where?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You cannot see her. But she sees you.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He swallowed hard. He looked around.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What is she doing?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}She is...\textquotedblright{}\textit{ I closed my eyes. I felt the weight inside me. The heat. The thing moving between my organs. }\textquotedblleft{}She is getting ready.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Ready for what?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}To leave.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Leave? Leave how?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You read the diary. You know how.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Dr. Oliveira went pale.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}She leaves through whoever reads.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Yes.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}No.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It is too late, Doctor. You read. She is inside you now.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He shook his head.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}No. I do not believe this. This is suggestion. It is your delusion. You put this idea in my head because...\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Because?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He did not finish.

\noindent The creature began to rise.

\noindent I felt her leaving the hole in my stomach. Rising through the esophagus. Filling the chest. The arms. The legs. The eyes.

\noindent My right eye burned.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Doctor\textquotedblright{}\textit{, I said. The voice was no longer entirely mine. }\textquotedblleft{}Doctor, get out of here.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Leave. Now.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He did not leave.

\noindent He kept looking at me while my body began to change. The skin of my face stretched. The teeth clenched. The yellow eye began to glow.

\noindent The creature smiled with my mouth.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You wanted to see, Doctor.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}...\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Then see.\textquotedblright{}}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent What happened next I do not remember well.

\noindent I remember a light. Very strong. I remember the sound of glass breaking. I remember the chair falling. I remember a voice screaming --- was it my voice? Was it his?

\noindent Then, darkness.

\noindent When I woke up, I was on the floor. The diary was open beside me. Dr. Oliveira was leaning against the wall, his eyes wide, his mouth open. He was not moving.

\noindent He was breathing.

\noindent But he was no longer there.

\noindent His eyes were empty.

\noindent The doctor who came into the room afterwards said I had a seizure. That Dr. Oliveira got a fright. That it was nothing.

\noindent But I saw.\NE{This passage describes the central mechanism of contamination of the narrative: the creature does not transfer through physical contact, but through textual mediation. The reader who follows Joaquim's account is, like Oliveira, exposed to the same vector of contagion. The tension between knowing and not knowing — between reading and being read — is the conflict the book proposes in every fragment. The participant of the reading is not a spectator: he is an accomplice.}

\noindent The creature had passed.

\noindent She was leaving me.

\noindent And entering him.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent I died three days later.

\noindent It was a Wednesday, June 12, 1979. My heart stopped. They said it was cardiac arrest. They said it was old age --- I was 72.

\noindent I know what it was.

\noindent The creature left. She took what was left of me.

\noindent The diary stayed.

\noindent They buried me with it. It stayed 62 days in the earth --- one day for each year of imprisonment.

\noindent When they exhumed me, the diary was intact.

\noindent The pages were still moist.

\noindent The ink still fresh.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The diary does not end when the author dies\textquotedblright{}\textit{, I wrote on the last page. }\textquotedblleft{}The diary begins when the reader opens it.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent You opened it.

\noindent The creature is reading with you.
