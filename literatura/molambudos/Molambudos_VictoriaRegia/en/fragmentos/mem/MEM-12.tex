\section*{\textbf{MEM-12} --- The Shroud-Ripper}
\markboth{MEM-12 --- The Shroud-Ripper}{}

\noindent \textit{The herald in the yard}

\noindent \textbf{1945 --- Barbacena Hospital-Colony}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent Twenty-eight years inside the Colony.

\noindent Twenty-eight years of peeling walls, of screams that become routine, of the smell of creolin that never leaves your nose. Twenty-eight years without feeling the wind on your face except through a barred window. Twenty-eight years of solitude that does not heal --- that petrifies.

\noindent In 1945, I was in the ward of the silenced. A ward where no one speaks because everyone learned that speaking does no good. I stayed curled in a corner, with the diary on my lap, writing without stopping. The creature inside me was quiet --- hibernating. I felt her weight, her warmth, her slow breathing.

\noindent Every now and then she would turn over inside my chest and I would feel a pang. It was not pain. It was \textit{remembrance}. She was reminding me she was there.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The Shroud-Ripper appeared one winter.

\noindent June 1945. The cold came in through the cracks in the wall and would not leave. I slept with my hands tucked under my armpits, trembling. The other patients trembled too. We trembled together, like animals in a pen, waiting for the slaughter.

\noindent One night, a sound woke me.

\noindent It was not a scream.
It was not crying.
It was a song.

\noindent Thin. High-pitched. A song that rose and fell like a woman weeping very far away.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Shroud-ripper...\textquotedblright{}}, someone whispered beside me.

\noindent It was Seu Nonô. An old man who had been in the Colony since 1920 --- 25 years interned. He pointed at the window with a trembling finger.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}She has arrived.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Who?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He did not answer. He only pointed.

\noindent On the windowsill, there was an owl.

\noindent She was white. Not white like snow --- white like old bone. Her feathers looked like light in the darkness, as if her body were made of condensed moonlight. Her eyes were black, round, without lids. She looked at me. Turned her head 180 degrees --- a slow, fluid movement that no bird should be able to make.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Shroud-ripper\textquotedblright{}\textit{, repeated Seu Nonô. }\textquotedblleft{}When she sings, someone dies. She rips the dead man's shroud with her beak so the soul can get out. That's where the name comes from.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The owl opened her beak.

\noindent The sound that came out was not a hoot.
It was a human scream.
A woman's scream --- but not of pain.
Of \textit{warning}.

\noindent The scream crossed the night. Crossed the bars. Crossed my chest.

\noindent The creature inside me \textit{woke}.

\noindent I felt her raise her head inside my body. Felt her eyes open --- the yellow eyes. She \textit{saw} the Shroud-Ripper.

\noindent And the Shroud-Ripper saw her.

\noindent The owl tilted her head. The scream stopped. A silence so absolute fell that I could hear my own blood moving.

\noindent And the Shroud-Ripper spoke.

\noindent Not with her own voice.

\noindent With the creature's voice.

\noindent The voice that came out of the hole inside me.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Not yet.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The owl spread her wings. They were enormous. White. They looked like a sheet floating in the dark.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}But soon.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She flew.

\noindent Her whiteness vanished into the night.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The next day, Seu Nonô was found dead in his bed.

\noindent His face was turned toward the window.
His mouth open.
His eyes open.

\noindent His eyelids had been torn out.

\noindent No blood remained. No marks. The eyelids had simply \textit{disappeared}. As if someone had taken them with surgical precision.

\noindent The orderlies said it was the rats.

\noindent I knew it was not.

\noindent The Shroud-Ripper had taken Seu Nonô's eyelids.
So that he would \textit{never close his eyes again}.
So that he would see what was coming.
Even after death.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The following week, the owl came back.

\noindent Every night, she perched on the windowsill and looked at me. She did not sing. She did not scream. She stayed there, motionless, her black eyes fixed on me.

\noindent And every night, the creature inside me spoke.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}She is my tongue\textquotedblright{}\textit{, she said. }\textquotedblleft{}My flying eye. My voice in the world of the living.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Why did she come?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}To see how you are doing.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And how am I doing?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Silence.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Ready.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Ready for what?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}To pass the diary on.\textquotedblright{}}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent After that night, I began to write in a different way.

\noindent The handwriting changed. The words came faster. I did not need to think --- my hand moved on its own, as if someone were guiding the pen.

\noindent The diary was no longer mine.

\noindent The creature was writing through me.

\noindent I was only the hand.
She was the voice.

\noindent And what she wrote --- I did not understand at the time. I only understood later. When I read it again. When I saw that the words were not for me.

\noindent They were for whoever read afterward.

\noindent They were for you.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent On the last night, the owl did not come.

\noindent I woke alone, in the dark cell, and I knew she had gone away.

\noindent But the creature inside me spoke:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}She went to prepare the way.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Prepare how?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The diary cannot stay here. It must get out. It must be found. It must be opened.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}By whom?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The creature laughed. The laugh echoed inside my chest.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Someone who needs me as much as you did.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And when will that happen?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}When you die.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And until then?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}We wait.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She fell quiet.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}We always wait.\textquotedblright{}}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent There were 34 more years of waiting.

\noindent 34 years listening to the Shroud-Ripper on the roofs of the Colony.

\noindent 34 years writing words that were not mine.

\noindent 34 years being the hand of a monster.

\noindent Until, in 1979, the new doctor arrived.

\noindent And the cycle began to close.
