\section*{\textbf{MEM-14} --- The Diary Emerges}
\markboth{MEM-14 --- The Diary Emerges}{}

\noindent \textit{Joaquim hands over his writings}

\noindent \textbf{1979 --- Joaquim hands over his writings}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent Dr. Oliveira appeared at the cell on a Monday morning.

\noindent I was sitting on the bed, with the diary on my lap, writing. When he opened the door, I closed the notebook.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Good morning, Joaquim.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Good morning.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He looked at the diary.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What is that?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Nothing.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It does not look like nothing. It looks like a notebook.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It is mine.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I know.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He sat on the bench in the cell --- something no doctor ever did. The doctors stood by the door, ready to leave. He sat down.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What do you write?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Things.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What things?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What I saw.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He waited.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What did you see, Joaquim?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I looked at him. With the yellow eye. He did not look away.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You will not believe it.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Try me.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I thought. Sixty-two years of silence. Sixty-two years keeping the story. Sixty-two years waiting for someone to ask.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Are you afraid of dying?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He was taken aback by the question.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}No... I do not think about it much.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Think.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Why?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Because you will die. Everyone dies. But not everyone truly dies.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What does that mean?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I held up the diary.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Do you want to read it?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He hesitated.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}May I?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You are the first one to ask. That is already more than all the others did.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent I held out the diary.

\noindent He took it.

\noindent The diary was warm. He felt it. I saw it in his eyes --- the shock of touching something that felt alive.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Start at the beginning\textquotedblright{}\textit{, I said. }\textquotedblleft{}1917. The year I came here.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He opened it.

\noindent And he began to read.

\noindent When he looked up, two hours had passed. He was pale. His hand was trembling.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Joaquim...\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Now you know.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Is this... true?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Did you smell the pages?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He did not answer.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Did you see the eye?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He looked at my right eye. It was glowing.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You read. Now you are part of it.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Part of what?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The Cycle.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He closed the diary. He stood up. He walked to the door.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I will come back tomorrow.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You will come back\textquotedblright{}\textit{, I said. }\textquotedblleft{}The creature will not let you leave.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He stopped.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What do you mean by that?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You read. She is in you now. It takes a few days to grow. But it grows.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He left without answering.

\noindent I heard his footsteps in the corridor. Fast. Disorderly.

\noindent The Cycle had begun.
