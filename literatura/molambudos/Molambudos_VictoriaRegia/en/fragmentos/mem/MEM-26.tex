\section*{\textbf{MEM-26} --- The Last Page}
\markboth{MEM-26 --- The Last Page}{}

\noindent \textit{3 days before dying}

\noindent \textbf{1979 --- Three days before dying}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent I knew I was going to die.

\noindent The body was shutting down. The heart was failing. The breathing was short. The creature was too heavy inside me.

\noindent Three days before dying, I wrote the last page.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Whoever reads this: you are next.}

\noindent \textit{I carried the creature for 62 years. She entered me when I was a child, in a trench, in the middle of a concentration camp. She ate me from the inside. But she also kept me alive.}

\noindent \textit{She needs a host. When I die, she will look for another.}

\noindent \textit{Not a body. A reader.}

\noindent \textit{You will read this diary. As you read, she will pass into you. Not physically. Spiritually. The story will enter your head. And, once inside, it never leaves.}

\noindent \textit{You will begin to dream of the trench. You will feel the smell. You will see the yellow eye in the corner of the room.}

\noindent \textit{You will think it is suggestion.}

\noindent \textit{It is not.}

\noindent \textit{It is the creature.}

\noindent \textit{Welcome to the Cycle, my substitute.}

\noindent \textit{--- Joaquim Antônio Correia}
\textit{--- Patient 1,260}
\textit{--- Last word: hunger.\textquotedblright{}}\NE{The editor records: the original page of this fragment was found loose in the inner pocket of Joaquim's jacket, during the 1979 exhumation. The paper was covered in dirt and moisture; the ink had smudged at various points, especially on the bottom margin. The transcription reproduced here is the legible text after restoration. The last word --- \textquotedblleft{}hunger\textquotedblright{} --- was almost illegible, preserved only by the pressure of the pen on the paper, which marked the following page even after the ink had dissolved.}
