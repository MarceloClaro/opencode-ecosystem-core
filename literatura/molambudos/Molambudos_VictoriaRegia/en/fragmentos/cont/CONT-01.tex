\section*{\textbf{CONT-01} --- You Opened the Diary}
\markboth{CONT-01 --- You Opened the Diary}{}

\noindent \textit{The first layer}

\noindent Do not pretend it was not your choice.

\noindent You could have closed the book in the bookstore. Could have run your finger along the spine and moved on to the next aisle. Could have read the back cover, thought \textquotedblleft{}interesting, but not today\textquotedblright{}, and put it back on the shelf.

\noindent But no.

\noindent You opened it.

\noindent There is a smell that comes from these pages. It is not old paper --- the book is new, it left the press weeks ago. But the smell is here, between the fibers of the paper. As if something had been printed along with the ink. Something that was \textit{waiting} to be breathed.

\noindent You raise the book to your nose without noticing. You only notice when you have already done it. Your heart has already raced before you understand why. The smell is sweet. Too sweetish. Like warm flowers. Like meat that is past its point but not yet spoiled. Like the breath of someone who has not eaten in days.

\noindent You did not return the book.

\noindent You bought it.

\noindent You brought it home.

\noindent Now you are reading this line.

\noindent \textit{--- Joaquim also wrote a diary. They buried it with him. For 62 years, the diary grew inside the earth, feeding on what remained of the body. When they exhumed him, the pages were still damp. The ink still fresh.}

\noindent \textit{The gravedigger said the diary was warm.}

\noindent You rested the book on your lap. Feel the weight. It is not a common weight. There is something \textit{inside} it that presses against your legs. A gravity that is not only physical.

\noindent Feel the pulse in your throat. Your breathing grew shorter. It is not fear --- not yet. It is the body recognizing, before the mind does, that something has changed.

\noindent Now you are going to read what Joaquim wrote.

\noindent And when you finish ---

\noindent --- you will understand that the diary was never his.

\noindent It was yours. All along.
