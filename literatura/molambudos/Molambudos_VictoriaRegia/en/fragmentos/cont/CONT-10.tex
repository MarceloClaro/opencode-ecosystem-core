\section*{\textbf{CONT-10} --- The Wound}
\markboth{CONT-10 --- The Wound}{}

\noindent \textit{The mark on the skin}

\noindent Did you feel it?

\noindent In the middle of the reading, an itch on your right arm. You ran your hand over it. Then you scratched. Then you looked.

\noindent There is a mark on your skin.

\noindent Small. Almost imperceptible. But it is there. A dark spot, like ink that leaked through from beneath the skin.

\noindent You rub it with your finger. The mark does not come off. You rub with saliva. It does not come off.

\noindent Feel it: your heart beats harder right at that spot on your skin, as if the mark had a pulse of its own.

\noindent The mark is beneath the skin.

\noindent It is \textit{growing}.

\noindent Not now. In the next few hours. Tomorrow morning, it will be bigger. The day after, it will have a shape.

\noindent The shape of a letter.

\noindent A crooked letter. Childish.

\noindent The first letter of Joaquim's name.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}J\textquotedblright{}.}

\noindent Tomorrow: \textit{\textquotedblleft{}J\textquotedblright{}}.
Then: \textit{\textquotedblleft{}JO\textquotedblright{}}.
The day after: \textit{\textquotedblleft{}JOA\textquotedblright{}}.

\noindent Until it forms: \textit{\textquotedblleft{}JOAQUIM\textquotedblright{}}.

\noindent On your skin.

\noindent Like a tattoo nobody made.
Like a mark that came from within.

\noindent Look again.

\noindent The creature is revealing itself.
Not in the book.
In you.
