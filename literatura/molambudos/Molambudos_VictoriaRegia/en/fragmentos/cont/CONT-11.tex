\section*{\textbf{CONT-11} --- The Number}
\markboth{CONT-11 --- The Number}{}

\noindent \textit{1,260 follows you}

\noindent 1260.

\noindent You saw this number today. Somewhere. On a license plate. In the change at the bakery. In the minute of the clock (12:60 does not exist, but your brain \textit{invented} 12:60 for a second).

\noindent The number is following you.

\noindent It is no coincidence. The number 1260 is the key. It is the code. It is the creature's signature.

\noindent 1260 days is the duration of the patient's cycle.
1260 was Joaquim's record.
Nothing else.

\noindent Now the number is in your field of vision.

\noindent You are going to see 1,260 in everything.
Hours. License plates. Pages. Codes.

\noindent And every time you see it, you will remember.
And every time you remember, you will smell it.
And, in that same instant, your heart will skip a beat.

\noindent Feel it now. It already skipped.

\noindent The number is the trigger.
The trigger is the recollection.
The recollection is the presence.

\noindent 1,260.

\noindent Done. Now it is in your head.

\noindent When you go to sleep tonight, it will be in your dream.
When you wake up, it will be in your peripheral vision.

\noindent 1,260.

\noindent 1,260.

\noindent 1,260.

\noindent The creature is counting. And you are the next number in the line.\NE{The editor notes: this fragment is not part of Joaquim's original manuscript. It was written in pencil on the edge of a loose page, in a handwriting that is neither Joaquim's, nor Oliveira's, nor Lúcia's. The page was found among the diary's sheets during the exhumation. The pencil was of a brand that did not exist in 1979. The editor chose to include it out of fidelity to the archive as a whole, without determining its authorship.}
