\section*{\textbf{CONT-09} --- The Mirror}
\markboth{CONT-09 --- The Mirror}{}

\noindent \textit{The reflection changed}

\noindent Go to a mirror.

\noindent Now.

\noindent It does not matter if it is the bathroom, the bedroom, or the reflection in a windowpane. Go to a mirror and look into your eyes.

\noindent Stare. For 30 seconds. Without blinking.

\noindent Notice how your eyes look different from how you remembered them. Is the iris lighter? Is there a yellowish tint that was not there before? Is the white of your eyes more... yellow?

\noindent Yes.

\noindent You are seeing it.

\noindent It is not your imagination. The change is subtle. Almost imperceptible. But it is there.

\noindent The right eye is changing.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent Run your hand over your face. Feel the temperature of your skin. Is it colder? Warmer? Is your hand trembling?

\noindent Your body is changing.

\noindent Not visibly --- internally. The smell you sensed is in your lungs. The yellow eye is on your retina. The creature's voice is in your auditory cortex.

\noindent You are no longer the person you were when you began reading.

\noindent The question is: when did the change begin?

\noindent Was it on the first page?
Was it in the first dream?
Was it now, while reading this instruction?

\noindent Or was it \textit{before}?

\noindent You dreamed of the yellow eye before you bought this book. Remember? It was one night, weeks ago. You woke up, found it strange, and forgot. Then you saw the book in the bookstore, and something made you buy it.

\noindent Something was already there.

\noindent Before the book.
Before the story.
Before you knew that a patient 1,260 existed.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The creature is not in the book.

\noindent The book is only the \textit{encounter}.

\noindent The creature was already in you. It was waiting for the book to \textit{awaken}.

\noindent The diary is not the source of the contamination.
The diary is the \textit{key}.
You were already contaminated before you opened it.
The diary only unlocked the door.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent Now, step away from the mirror.

\noindent Look at your hands. Study the palms. The lines. The texture of the skin.

\noindent Now close your eyes and imagine Joaquim's hands. Thin. Trembling. Full of calluses from writing.

\noindent Open your eyes.

\noindent Are your hands the same as his?

\noindent No. Of course not. The hands are different.

\noindent But the resemblance will grow.

\noindent Not physically --- spiritually. You now carry the same story. The same memory. The same hunger.

\noindent Your hands are not Joaquim's hands.

\noindent But, when you write, the handwriting will be similar.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent \textit{--- Go to the mirror.}

\noindent \textit{--- Look for 30 seconds.}

\noindent \textit{--- Ask: \textquotedblleft{}Who is looking back at me?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{--- The answer will change every time you ask.}

\noindent \textit{--- Until, one time, it will not be you.}

\noindent \textit{--- It will be him.}

\noindent \textit{--- Welcome to the cycle, Patient 1,263.}

\noindent \textit{--- The mirror knows. The mirror always knew.}
