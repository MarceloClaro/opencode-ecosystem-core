\section*{\textbf{CONT-02} --- You Were There}
\markboth{CONT-02 --- You Were There}{}

\noindent \textit{You always were}

\noindent You think you are reading about the past.

\noindent You think Joaquim is a character. That the trench is a scene. That the yellow eye is a metaphor for trauma. That the creature is a projection of his mind, a psychological defense, a mechanism of dissociation.

\noindent You are wrong.

\noindent You were in that trench.

\noindent Not physically. You were not born in 1907, you did not walk the Sertão of Quixeramobim, you did not watch your father die of hunger. All of that is true.

\noindent But you were there in another way.

\noindent You were there because Joaquim's story is the story of everyone who has ever felt hunger. Not hunger for food. Hunger for something the world does not give. Hunger for meaning. Hunger for affection. Hunger for justice. Hunger to be seen.

\noindent You have felt that hunger.

\noindent You have felt alone in a crowd. You have had the feeling that there is something wrong inside you. Something that has no name. Something you feed with distractions, with work, with noise, with anything that keeps the silence away. You have woken in the middle of the night with the feeling that you are not alone in the room.

\noindent Feel the cold rise from the back of your neck to your scalp. Your stomach clenches a second before you remember why.

\noindent The creature Joaquim found in the trench is not a supernatural entity.

\noindent It is the hunger everyone carries. The difference is that Joaquim \textit{saw} it. Touched it. Let it in.

\noindent It is already in you too.

\noindent You just have not seen it yet.

\noindent But after this book, you will begin to see it.

\noindent In the corners of the room.

\noindent In the reflection of the glass.

\noindent In the yellow eye of the dog in the street.

\noindent And one night, when you are lying down, with the light off, you will feel a weight on the back of your neck. A warm breath.

\noindent Don't move.

\noindent You will not be afraid. You will recognize it.

\noindent You will think: \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You were there too.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent And the creature will answer:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I never left.\textquotedblright{}}
