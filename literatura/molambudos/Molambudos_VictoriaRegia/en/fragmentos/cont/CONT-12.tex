\section*{\textbf{CONT-12} --- 3:14}
\markboth{CONT-12 --- 3:14}{}

\noindent \textit{The hour of the cycle}

\noindent 3:14.

\noindent It is the hour.

\noindent You did not know this until now, but 3:14 in the morning is the cycle's magical hour. It is when Joaquim woke. It is when Oliveira woke. It is when Lúcia woke. It is when you will wake.

\noindent Do not fail.

\noindent Tonight, when you go to sleep, leave the clock visible. Set yourself to wake at 3:14.

\noindent You will wake up.

\noindent Not because you set the alarm --- because the creature will wake you. It wants you to \textit{take part} in the ritual. Waking at 3:14 is the first step of acceptance.

\noindent When you wake, do not turn on the light.

\noindent Stay in the dark.

\noindent Count to 60.

\noindent If you reach 60 without seeing anything, you may go back to sleep.

\noindent If you do not reach it --- if you see a yellow glow in the corner of the room before 60 --- then the cycle is complete.

\noindent You saw.

\noindent You know.

\noindent You accepted.

\noindent 3:14.

\noindent Tonight.

\noindent Do not fail.
