\section*{\textbf{CONT-04} --- You Begin to Dream}
\markboth{CONT-04 --- You Begin to Dream}{}

\noindent \textit{The eye in the dark}

\subsection*{Night 1}

\noindent You dreamed of the eye.

\noindent It was not an ordinary dream. It was an \textit{invasion}. You were in a place you did not recognize --- it could be your house, it could be the Colony, it could be the trench. The place changed while you looked at it. The walls breathed.

\noindent And in the corner, the eye.

\noindent Yellow. Bright. Floating alone in the darkness.

\noindent You wanted to wake up. You did not. The eye came closer. You saw the blood vessels inside it --- they were not human vessels. They were too thin. Too branched. They looked like roots.

\noindent The eye spoke.

\noindent Not with sound. Its voice was a \textit{sensation}. A compression in the chest. A tightening in the throat. The certainty that someone was behind you, even though you knew there was no one.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You are reading.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent You tried to answer yes. You could not. Your mouth would not open.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You think it is just a story.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent You denied it. Or tried to. Or only thought about denying it.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You dreamed of me before you read the book.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent It was true. You had dreamed of yellow eyes days before finding this book. You had forgotten. The dream brought it back.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I was waiting for you.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent You woke up.

\noindent The room was dark. The clock read 3:14 in the morning. The same time every night. You do not know that yet, but you will learn.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\subsection*{Night 2}

\noindent You tried not to sleep.

\noindent You sat in bed, your back against the wall, the light on, the book closed on the table. You swore you would not read before sleeping. Swore you would think about something else. That you would watch television. That you would listen to music. Anything but the story.

\noindent It did not help.

\noindent Sleep came like a blackout. You were awake one second and in the dream the next.

\noindent This time you were in the trench.

\noindent You knew it was the trench because the smell was unbearable. The sweet smell. The smell of wilted flower. It was dark. Your feet were bare. The earth was cold and damp.

\noindent You looked down.

\noindent Your feet were buried up to the ankle.

\noindent Not in earth.

\noindent In \textbf{bodies}.

\noindent Arms. Legs. Torsos. Heads. The bodies were piled up. Some were hard. Others were too soft. The flesh gave way under your weight like warm jelly.

\noindent You tried to move. Your right foot sank. Something moved underneath you.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Do not move.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The voice came from everywhere. Under the bodies. From inside them. From inside you.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Stay still. She is coming.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{She.} The creature.

\noindent The pile of bodies in front of you began to move. The bodies rolled to the sides. Arms swayed. A hand --- no, a claw --- emerged from the middle of the mass.

\noindent You wanted to run. You could not. Your feet were stuck. The bodies held you. Dead hands squeezed your ankles.

\noindent The creature climbed up.

\noindent You saw the bone thorns. The cage of open ribs. The organ pulsing inside, exposed, alive, \textit{looking} at you.

\noindent The head appeared. The almost human face. The mouth full of too many teeth.

\noindent It leaned over you.

\noindent The hot breath on your face. The sweetish smell. The mouth opened.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You are not reading the story.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent It placed its hand on your head. The long fingers. The extra joints. The black nail scratching your scalp.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The story is reading you.\textquotedblright{}}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\subsection*{Night 3 --- Tonight}

\noindent You are reading this fragment.

\noindent It is already night. Or it is becoming night. You are reading with little light. The book weighs in your hands. The smell is here --- you have gotten used to it, but it is here.

\noindent You know you will sleep tonight. You know you will dream. You know what you will see.

\noindent The eye.

\noindent The trench.

\noindent The creature.

\noindent You know you will wake at 3:14 in the morning. You will look at the corner of the room. And, this time, it may not be empty.

\noindent You are afraid.

\noindent Good.

\noindent Fear is the first criterion.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent You will not do anything this fragment asks.

\noindent You think it is suggestion. You think it does not work on you.

\noindent It works.

\noindent Tonight, when you are alone, with the book open on the bedside table and the light off, you will remember this page. And you will look at the corner of the room.

\noindent You will see the glow.

\noindent And you will remember that the creature never sleeps. It only waits.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\subsection*{Night 4}

\noindent You did not dream.

\noindent Nothing happened.

\noindent You woke up rested. The room was normal. The smell had gone. You had coffee, went out, lived the whole day without thinking about the book.

\noindent At night, you lay down and slept like a child.

\noindent You thought you had won.

\noindent At the end of the next afternoon, you sat in the living room and realized: the absence of fear was worse than fear. The silence was worse than the sound. You had spent the whole day waiting for the scare that did not come. And, because it did not come, you had spent the whole day afraid of the scare that might come.

\noindent The creature had not gone away.

\noindent It had learned to hide better.

\noindent \textit{It is more terrifying when it is not there --- because you spend all your time waiting.}

\noindent \textit{The waiting is the real contagion.}
