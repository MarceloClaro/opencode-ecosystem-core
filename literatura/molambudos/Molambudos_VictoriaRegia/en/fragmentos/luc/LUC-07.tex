\section*{\textbf{LUC-07} --- Oliveira's Trail}
\markboth{LUC-07 --- Oliveira's Trail}{}

\noindent \textit{Following the cycle in reverse}

\noindent \textbf{April 2026 --- Belo Horizonte / Quixeramobim}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent After reading Oliveira's letter, Lúcia did what any forensic psychiatrist would do: she followed the trail.

\noindent Dr. Heitor Oliveira disappeared on June 13, 1979. His car was found three weeks later in a parking lot in Quixeramobim, Ceará. The gas had run out. The doors were unlocked. Inside, a backpack with clothes, a notebook with 32 written pages (which no one had read), and a bus ticket to Barbacena dated June 17 --- the same day he arrived.

\noindent He had come back.

\noindent Lúcia spent an afternoon at the Public Archive, digging through boxes. She found Oliveira's notebook --- forgotten, dusty, with the label \textquotedblleft{}Belongings of Dr. H. Oliveira --- 1979\textquotedblright{}.

\noindent She read the 32 pages in 40 minutes.

\noindent They were clinical notes, but with a difference: the handwriting changed across the pages. It began firm, medical. It ended trembling, irregular, \textit{childish}.

\noindent The last sentence was:

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I came back to Barbacena. The cycle will not let me go. I return the diary to the archive. I hope the next reader understands better than I did.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textbf{The next reader.}

\noindent Her.

\noindent She was the next one.

\noindent The line had reached her.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent Lúcia spent the night at the archive.

\noindent She read Oliveira's diary. She read the session reports. She read the letter Oliveira had left for her --- written in 1979, delivered in 2026.

\noindent And, for the second time, she read Joaquim's diary.

\noindent Except that, this time, she read it with different eyes.

\noindent Not as a psychiatrist.
As an \textit{heir}.

\noindent Every word entered. Every sentence echoed. She felt the sweetish smell rise from the pages. Her right eye began to burn.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The cycle has no return\textquotedblright{}}, said a voice inside her.

\noindent It was not her voice.

\noindent She closed the diary.

\noindent Put it in her backpack.

\noindent And, for the first time, she was certain she would not be able to stop.
