\section*{\textbf{LUC-Contraprova} --- The Counterproof}
\markboth{LUC-Contraprova --- The Counterproof}{}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}This has to have a scientific explanation\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textbf{April 2026 --- Belo Horizonte}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent Three days after the dream, Lúcia Mendes walked into the laboratory of the UFMG Clinical Hospital with a folder full of tests.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I need a complete battery\textquotedblright{}\textit{, she told the technician. }\textquotedblleft{}Brain MRI with contrast. Retinal mapping. 24-hour electroencephalogram. Complete blood count. Hormone panel. Liver profile. Thyroid function test. RPR. ANA. Toxoplasmosis. Cytomegalovirus. Rapid HIV test. Syphilis. Everything.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The technician raised an eyebrow. \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What do you have?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I do not know. I want to find out.\textquotedblright{}}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The results came a week later.

\noindent \textbf{Magnetic resonance imaging:} Normal. No lesions, no tumors, no signal changes in the brain parenchyma. Hypothalamus, amygdala, hippocampus --- all structures within normal patterns.

\noindent \textbf{Electroencephalogram:} Normal electrical activity. No epileptiform discharges. No patterns of focal slow activity. No evidence of epileptogenic activity in the temporal lobe.

\noindent \textbf{Retinal mapping:\textit{ Retinal pigment epithelium normal. Optic disc without changes. Intraocular pressure normal. }\textquotedblleft{}Your right eye is healthy\textquotedblright{}\textit{, said the ophthalmologist. }\textquotedblleft{}The discoloration has no detectable organic cause.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textbf{Blood tests:} All within normal limits. Glucose, urea, creatinine, sodium, potassium, calcium, magnesium --- normal. Thyroid hormones --- normal. Serologies --- all negative.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You are healthy\textquotedblright{}\textit{, said the doctor, closing the chart. }\textquotedblleft{}The eye... well, it happens sometimes. Idiopathic acquired heterochromia. There is no explanation, but it does not kill anyone.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent --- \textit{There is no explanation.}

\noindent Lúcia stored the words away. She left the hospital carrying the folder of tests. Crossed the street. Sat on a park bench. Opened the folder. Read every report again.

\noindent All normal.

\noindent She was healthy.

\noindent Then why did the dream not stop? Why did the sweetish smell appear at random hours? Why did she wake up every night at 3:14?

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}There is no explanation.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent She closed the folder. Looked at the sky. The sky was blue, but she could not ignore the yellow stain in her peripheral vision.

\noindent --- \textbf{There is no explanation.}

\noindent That was when she decided: if science could not answer, she would go to the place where it all began.
