\section*{\textbf{LUC-05} --- Lúcia's Dream}
\markboth{LUC-05 --- Lúcia's Dream}{}

\noindent \textit{The contamination of the researcher}

\noindent \textbf{April 2026 --- Belo Horizonte}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent She dreamed of the trench.

\noindent Not like someone watching a film. Like someone who \textit{is} there. Bare feet on the cold earth. The sweetish smell entering through the pores. The dark sky, without stars, without moon, as if the world had forgotten to turn on the lights.

\noindent She heard the chewing before she saw the creature.

\noindent The sound of bone being broken. Of flesh being ground. A wet sound, patient, rhythmic --- like someone eating slowly because there is no hurry, because the food will not run away.

\noindent She walked to the edge of the trench.

\noindent Down below, the pile of bodies moved.

\noindent The creature raised its head.

\noindent The almost human face. Gray skin stretched over the bones. The mouth dirty with blood. The yellow eyes shining in the dark.

\noindent It smiled.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Finally\textquotedblright{}\textit{, said the creature. }\textquotedblleft{}I was waiting for you.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You knew I would come?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I have always known. Since the first page.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The creature climbed up. The immense body, the cage of exposed ribs, the organ pulsing between the bones. It stopped in front of Lúcia.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You read the diary.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}...Yes.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What did you think?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}It is the saddest story I have ever read.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The creature tilted its head.

\noindent \textquotedblleft{\textit{It is not sad. It is true. You confuse the two things.\textquotedblright{}}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What do you want from me?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Nothing. I already have what I wanted.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The creature touched Lúcia's chest with a long finger. The finger crossed the skin, the bones. It did not hurt. It simply... entered.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Now you are mine.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}No\textquotedblright{}\textit{, said Lúcia. }\textquotedblleft{}I am not.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You already are. From the moment you opened the diary. You just have not accepted it yet.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Lúcia woke up.

\noindent The room was dark. The clock read 3:14.
\noindent The rotten sweetness was on the pillow.

\noindent Inside, the cell was empty. Almost.

\noindent She sat up in bed, panting. She looked at the corner of the room. The shadow seemed denser.

\noindent She turned on the light.

\noindent Nothing.

\noindent But the smell did not go away.
\noindent She sat up in bed, panting. She looked at the corner of the room. The shadow seemed denser. And there was a sound. A sound she knew from Joaquim's report --- a thin, high-pitched song, like a woman weeping very far away. The song of an owl. The Shroud-Ripper. She did not see the bird. But she heard it. She heard the wings opening, the beating in the still air of the room.

\noindent She sat in bed, with the light on, until the sun rose.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The next morning, she went to the ophthalmologist.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Your right eye has a slight discoloration\textquotedblright{}\textit{, said the doctor. }\textquotedblleft{}Nothing serious. Just a change in the pigmentation of the iris. Were you born with it?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}No.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Hmm. Could be medication. Do you take any medicine?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}No.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textquotedblleft{\textit{Strange.\textquotedblright{}} He wrote something in the chart. }\textquotedblleft{\textit{I will order some tests. Just to be sure.\textquotedblright{}}}

\noindent Lúcia looked in the office mirror.

\noindent The right eye was lighter.
Yellowish.

\noindent She said nothing.

\noindent But, on the way out, she ran her hand over her face and felt the skin colder than it should be.

\noindent The cycle had begun.
