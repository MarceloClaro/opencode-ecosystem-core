\section*{\textbf{LUC-09} --- Oliveira's Exhumation}
\markboth{LUC-09 --- Oliveira's Exhumation}{}

\noindent \textit{47 years later, the skull in the trench}

\noindent \textbf{June 2026 --- Quixeramobim, Ceará}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent Lúcia went to Ceará on June 13, 2026. Exactly 47 years after Joaquim's death and Oliveira's disappearance.

\noindent She did not know what she expected to find. The trench was there --- the clearing of black earth, the crooked tree, the sweetish smell rising from the ground as if the earth were breathing.
\noindent She buried her hands in the black earth.

\noindent She knelt.

\noindent She brought her face closer.
\noindent A yellow reflection, faint, at the bottom of the orbit.

\noindent The earth was warm.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Dr. Oliveira?\textquotedblright{}\textit{, she called. }\textquotedblleft{}Are you here?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The wind answered.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I read your diary. I read Joaquim's. I know what happened. I am feeling the same. The eye. The smell. The dreams. It is happening to me too.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textquotedblleft{\textit{Dr. Oliveira?\textquotedblright{}}}, she called. \textquotedblleft{\textit{Are you here?\textquotedblright{}}}
\noindent \textquotedblleft{\textit{I am going to hand the original over for judicial custody and keep only partial transcriptions. No one needs to touch the diary again.\textquotedblright{}}}
\noindent \textquotedblleft{\textit{Is that a clinical decision or superstition?\textquotedblright{}}}
\noindent At the door, before leaving, she left a copy of the report on Regina's desk.
\noindent \textquotedblleft{\textit{If I disappear, give this to the Council. Do not read the diary. Read only the report.\textquotedblright{}}}

\noindent And she began to dig.

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent She dug for two hours.

\noindent The earth was loose, oily. Easy to dig. As if it had been turned over many times.

\noindent At one meter deep, the shovel struck something hard.

\noindent She dug with her hands.

\noindent It was a bone.

\noindent Not an animal's. Human.

\noindent She dug more. A rib. A vertebra. A skull.

\noindent The skull had a crack on the right temple. As if from a pointed object.

\noindent And inside the right orbit --- the orbit was still dark, still holding traces of tissue --- something glinted.

\noindent She took a shovel from her backpack.

\noindent A yellow reflection.

\noindent The eye still shone.

\noindent Even after 47 years.

\noindent Lúcia sat on the floor of the trench, with Oliveira's skull in her hand, the taste of burnt sugar in her lungs, and a certainty rising up her ankle.
\noindent And something had just touched her.

\noindent It was a chain of bodies.

\noindent And she was the next one.
