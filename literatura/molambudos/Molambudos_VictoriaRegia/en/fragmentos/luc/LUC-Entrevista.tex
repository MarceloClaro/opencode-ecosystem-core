\section*{\textbf{LUC-Entrevista} --- The Interview}
\markboth{LUC-Entrevista --- The Interview}{}

\noindent \textit{The man who saw the cycle begin}

\noindent \textbf{April 2026 --- Barbacena, Mr. Antenor's house}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The address was in the Colony archive, written in pencil on the edge of a 1958 report: \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Antenor Francisco da Silva, former nurse, Rua da Matriz 42, Barbacena.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Lúcia knocked on the door at 10 in the morning. A very old man opened it. He must have been over 90 --- his skin thin as tissue paper, his blue eyes faded, his hands trembling.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Are you the psychiatrist?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I am.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Then you have read the diary.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent It was not a question.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}...I have.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Old Antenor opened the door slowly: \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Then come in. Before the smell comes in with you.\textquotedblright{}}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The house was small, dark, smelling of mold and medicine. On the living room wall, a yellowed photograph: men in white coats in front of the Hospital-Colony, 1950s.

\noindent Antenor served coffee. The cup trembled in his hand.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What do you want to know?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Everything you know about patient 1,260.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Joaquim.\textquotedblright{}\textit{ He said the name the way one says the name of a dead relative. }\textquotedblleft{}I knew him. I knew them all. But Joaquim was different.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Different how?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}He was not crazy. You read the diary. You know he was not crazy.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I know.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}He saw things.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What kind of things?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}The creature. He saw the creature. Since 1915. Before entering the Colony. He already saw it.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Antenor told Joaquim's story the way one tells a story heard many times. Joaquim was not violent when he arrived --- it was the trench that made him violent. The trench where the unburied bodies were eaten by animals. The trench where Joaquim saw the creature for the first time.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}He told me once: 'The creature did not come from outside. It was already in the trench. Buried together with the bodies. I just... woke it up.'\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}And you believed him?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Antenor looked at Lúcia. The faded blue eyes, but absolute lucidity.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I worked 42 years at the Colony. I saw patients have seizures. I saw patients being lobotomized. I saw patients die of hunger. I saw things you would not believe if I told you. But of all the things I saw, the only one I could not explain was Joaquim's diary.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What do you mean?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He stood up. Went to a cupboard. Took out a shoe box. Opened it.

\noindent She thought she would find photos, documents, memories.

\noindent Instead, she found a notebook. Black cover. Yellowed pages. Trembling handwriting.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Is this...\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Joaquim's second diary.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Lúcia felt her blood run cold.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}He wrote two?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You read the first one. The one he wrote alive. This one he wrote dead.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}How?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I dug his grave. Three days after the burial, the grave had been disturbed. I opened it. The coffin was broken from the inside. The notebook was on his chest, with his hand on top of it. The handwriting was the same. But the date was after death.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent He handed the notebook to Lúcia.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You can keep it. I have been afraid to death of it my whole life. It is time to pass it on. That is how it works, is it not?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Lúcia held the notebook. The cover was cold. But, inside it, something warmed up.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}That is how it works\textquotedblright{}}, she repeated.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Yes\textquotedblright{}\textit{, said Antenor. }\textquotedblleft{}You already know. You just have not accepted it yet.\textquotedblright{}}
