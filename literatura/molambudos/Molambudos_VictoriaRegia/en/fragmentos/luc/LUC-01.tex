\section*{\textbf{LUC-01} --- The Invitation}
\markboth{LUC-01 --- The Invitation}{}

\noindent \textit{Winter 2026 --- Belo Horizonte}

\noindent \textbf{Winter 2026 --- Belo Horizonte}

\vspace{0.5em}\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.5pt}\vspace{0.5em}

\noindent The e-mail arrived on a Tuesday, 8:47 in the morning.

\noindent Dr. Lúcia Mendes was in her office, a cup of coffee cooling on the desk and three reports to review. She clicked on the sender: \textit{Public Archive of Minas Gerais --- Historical Documents Section}.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Dear Dr. Lúcia,}

\noindent \textit{As we discussed in January, we have located the complete medical record of Patient 1,260 (Hospital-Colony of Barbacena) and the attachments mentioned: reports by Dr. Heitor Oliveira, the forensic autopsy report from the IML, and the patient's diary.}

\noindent \textit{The documents are available for consultation in our archive. Given your academic interest in the case, we have set aside a study room.}

\noindent \textit{Sincerely,}
\textit{Dr. Ronaldo Torres --- Director of the Public Archive of Minas Gerais\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Lúcia read the e-mail three times.

\noindent She had requested access to the archive in January --- routine research for an article on dissociative disorders in institutionalized populations. The Colony case was famous in Brazilian forensic psychiatry: sixty thousand dead in eighty years, patients buried alive, manufactured diagnoses.

\noindent But Patient 1,260 was different.

\noindent She had found a mention of the case in a footnote of someone's doctoral thesis --- one line: \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Patient 1,260, Joaquim Antônio Correia, admitted in 1917, died in 1979. Diary found with the body at the exhumation. Content not analyzed.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Content not analyzed.

\noindent That intrigued her. What could a Colony patient have written that \textit{nobody} had analyzed in nearly fifty years?

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}You are going to regret this\textquotedblright{}}, said the voice in her head. It was not a supernatural voice. It was the voice of experience. Fifteen years of forensic psychology had taught her that some archives should remain closed.

\noindent She picked up the phone.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Public Archive of Minas Gerais, good morning.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Dr. Lúcia Mendes. Confirm my appointment to consult record 1,260.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}One moment...\textquotedblright{}\textit{ Keys. }\textquotedblleft{}Dr. Lúcia, Mr. Ronaldo left a note here. He asks that you call him before you come. He said it is... urgent.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Urgent?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Yes. He said: 'Tell Dr. Lúcia that the diary is no longer in the archive.'\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Lúcia held the phone more tightly.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}What do you mean, no longer?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}He will explain in person. But he asked me to tell you: the diary was removed from the archive in 1979. By a physician. Dr. Heitor Oliveira. The same one who signs the reports attached to the record.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Removed when?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Two days before he disappeared.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent The coffee on Lúcia's desk had gone completely cold.

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}I am on my way there now.\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}Dr. Lúcia...\textquotedblright{}\textit{ The attendant's voice hesitated. }\textquotedblleft{}Mr. Ronaldo said something else. He said: 'Tell her the diary was returned last week. By a person who did not identify herself. Returned in person. On the reception desk. Wrapped in a black cloth.'\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}...And?\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent \textit{\textquotedblleft{}She said: 'Tell Dr. Lúcia that the diary is waiting for her.'\textquotedblright{}}

\noindent Lúcia hung up.

\noindent She looked at the cold coffee.
Looked at the reports.
Looked at the clock.

\noindent 8:52.

\noindent Five minutes after the e-mail, she already knew something was wrong.

\noindent She just did not know \textit{how wrong}.
